## Running Session — 7 km Peak Run

**Context:** Week 4 Saturday run was cut short at 3 km. Today's session completes the full distance — 7 km rather than the original 6 km — to bank a clean long-run close before Cycle 5.

### Warm-up (5 min)
- 3 min easy walk/jog (RPE 3)
- Leg swings (front-to-back and lateral) × 10/side
- Ankle circles × 10/side
- Calf raises × 15
- High knees × 20 (easy, in place)

### Main Run (35–40 min)

- **Option A — Continuous run: 7 km @ ~5:15–5:20/km**
  - First 1 km: easy @ ~5:45/km — warm the legs up, especially after Friday's back squat session
  - km 2–5: steady @ 5:15–5:20/km — RPE 7, strong and controlled; able to speak short phrases but not hold a full conversation
  - km 6–7: push to 5:00–5:10/km if legs allow — close the cycle strong
  - Target total time: ~37–39 min
- **Option B — Intervals: 6 × 1 km @ ~5:00/km**
  - Start with 1 km easy @ 5:45/km before the intervals begin
  - Rest 90 s easy walk between each interval rep
  - RPE per interval: 8 — controlled hard effort; aim for even splits across all 6 reps; do not go sub-4:55 on rep 1
  - Target: all 6 reps at 5:00–5:10/km
- **Indoor alternative:** 35 min steady effort on rower or bike at RPE 6–7 (Zone 3)

### Cool-down (5 min)
- 3 min easy walk
- Wall calf stretch 2 × 45 s/side
- Seated single-leg hamstring fold 45 s/side
- Kneeling hip flexor stretch 45 s/side

### Score
- Option chosen: A
- Distance: 7 km | Total time: 43 min 10 s
- Avg pace: 6:10/km | RPE: 6
