## Main Row

### 4 × 1500 m Intervals
- **4 × 1500 m** @ ≤2:22/500 m per rep
- Rest: 2:30 easy row or walk between reps (rate ~16 spm)
- Stroke rate during intervals: 22–24 spm
- RPE per interval: 8–8.5 — hard, sustained, controlled

**Pacing structure:**
- Rep 1: settle at 2:20–2:22 — conservative start; establish rhythm in the first 300 m; do not go sub-2:18 on rep 1; 1500 m at 2:22/500 m ≈ 7:06
- Rep 2: hold 2:19–2:21 — consistency is the primary objective; even splits across reps is more valuable than a fast rep 1 followed by a slow rep 4
- Rep 3: maintain 2:18–2:21 — if breathing is controlled and legs feel good, hold or nudge the split down slightly
- Rep 4: push to 2:16–2:18 if form and breathing allow — close the cycle's final programmed row intervals with the fastest piece

**Context from W4:** W4 row (3×2000 m) produced splits of 2:10, 2:14, and 2:15/500 m at an average of 19 spm — significantly faster than the ≤2:28 target. Today's 4×1500 m at ≤2:22/500 m is well within demonstrated capacity. The shorter pieces (1500 m vs 2000 m) allow slightly higher intensity; targeting 22–24 spm pairs technique with output better than the 19 spm in W4.

**Technique reminders:**
- Drive phase: legs push first, hips open second, arms pull last — at 2:20/500 m pace this sequence degrades under fatigue; stay deliberate
- Power per stroke at 22 spm is more sustainable than sloppy pulls at 26 spm; do not let stroke rate creep as the split slips
- Breathe out forcefully on each drive; recover nasal on the return slide where possible
