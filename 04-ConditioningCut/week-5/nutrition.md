Week 5 is the **Peak & Test** extension of the 04-ConditioningCut cycle — added to complete Week 4's skipped Friday session and the Saturday run that was cut short at 3 km (bathroom emergency). ## Key Timing Reminders

- **Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri — 60–90 min before):** 400–500 kcal with 60–75 g carbs + 30–40 g protein, fat <15 g; 500 ml water on waking; activate the +30 g carbs option on Monday (87.5 kg front squat + BW+10 kg pull-ups) and Friday (107.5 kg back squat + HSPU volume cluster)
- **Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri — within 45 min):** 40–50 g protein + 60–70 g carbs; keep fat minimal in this window
- **Pre-row (Tue — 60 min before):** 400–500 kcal with 60–70 g carbs + 25–35 g protein; 4×1500 m intervals demand consistent glycogen — do not under-fuel this session
- **Pre-run (Sat or Sun — 60–90 min before):** 300–450 kcal with 50–65 g carbs + 20–30 g protein; 7 km at tempo pace (~5:15/km) requires adequate glycogen; both Option A and Option B should be fuelled the same way

## Recovery & Deficit Notes

- **Week 5 is a cycle extension, not a new cycle:** All deficit protocols from Weeks 1–4 remain in place. Do not increase calories because the week was added — the body has adapted to the 2500 kcal target.
- **Pre-workout carb option:** Monday (+10 kg pull-up load + HSPU max-rep test) and Friday (107.5 kg back squat) are the two heaviest sessions of the week — activating the +30 g pre-workout option on both is recommended.
- **Friday note:** Friday's metcon (DU + hang power snatches + ring dips) was originally programmed for Week 4 and skipped. Pre-fuel it the same as any other FF session — standard pre-workout timing applies.
- **Ankle monitoring:** Monday HSPU max-rep test — cap total wall time at 8 min; note any ankle irritation in the score block and factor into Cycle 5 programming decisions.
- **Scale tracking:** One additional week at deficit means one more shot at the 0.5–0.75 kg/week loss target. If the scale shows no movement after 5 full weeks of consistent deficit, plan a 1–2 week maintenance break before Cycle 5 rather than deepening the cut.
- **Cycle-end benchmarks:** After Sunday, compile the performance table in the Sunday file (front squat, deadlift, back squat, weighted pull-up, HSPU, row, run) — these numbers seed Cycle 5 loading.
- **Hydration:** 3.5–4 L water daily baseline; add ~500 ml per training hour; electrolytes (sodium 500–700 mg/L + potassium) on all five training days; extra attention on Saturday's 7 km run — longer duration means higher sweat losses.
