## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 8–10 rounds)

- **25 double unders** (alt: 60 single unders) — sole cardio modality
- **7 hang power snatches** @ 42.5 kg
- **10 ring dips**

*This metcon was programmed for Week 4 Friday (2026-07-25) and was not completed — running it now provides the cycle's final metcon data point.*

**Strategy:** Double unders at a relaxed, rhythmic pace — 25 reps should take ~20–25 s; smooth consistent rhythm, do not sprint the rope and burn out. Hang power snatches as fast singles — pull from the hang, shrug tall, punch overhead to a locked-out catch; 42.5 kg should feel fast and technical; if the bar drifts forward on the catch or the elbow bends, lower to 40 kg immediately. Ring dips unbroken for the first 5 rounds (rings slightly turned out at the bottom, full lockout at the top); split 6+4 when the push-out starts to slow; if ring dips are not available, substitute 10 bar dips or 12 push-up-to-downward-dog.

**Alt (no rope):** Replace 25 DU with 10 box jump overs (24").
