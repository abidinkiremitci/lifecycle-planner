## Strength (20 min)

- **Front Squat 4 × 4 @ ~82% (~85 kg)**, rest 2:30
  - Cues: elbows high and forward before descent, brace hard before the hole, knees out aggressively, lead with elbows on the drive up — do not let the chest drop forward
  - Note: +5 kg from Week 3 — this is the heaviest front squat of the cycle; rep 4 of sets 3 and 4 should be a focused grind, not a failure attempt; if the chest collapses on any rep, lower to 82.5 kg and log it
- **Accessory — weighted strict pull-ups: 4 × 5 @ BW + 7.5 kg**, rest 2:00
  - Cues: dead hang start, full scapular depression before pulling, chin clearly over bar, 2 s controlled lower — no kip; keep the weight stable (KB between ankles or weight belt)
  - Note: +2.5 kg from Week 3's +5 kg; if rep 5 fails on sets 3–4, complete remaining reps at +5 kg and record both loads; preserving 4 sets matters more than the extra load

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 9–12 rounds)

- **15 box jump overs** (24" box) — sole cardio modality
- **8 hang squat cleans** @ 57.5 kg
- **14 KB swings** @ 24 kg (Russian — eye-level)

**Strategy:** Box jump overs at a steady pace — two feet take off, land on the far side, step or jump back; aim ~3 s per rep (15 reps ≈ 45 s); do not rush and lose your footing. Hang squat cleans as fast singles — start from the hang, pull high, drop under and catch in the squat; reset at the hip between reps; 57.5 kg should feel powerful in rounds 1–4 and demanding by round 8. KB swings unbroken throughout — drive hips hard, bell reaches eye level, control the hike back; do not bend the arms at the top.

**Loading note:** If the squat clean catch position starts to collapse (elbows dropping, torso folding) from round 5, drop to 55 kg and preserve the mechanics.

**Alt (no box):** Replace box jump overs with 12 burpee lateral jumps over the barbell.

## Skill / Core (13 min)

**HSPU — direct wall work (8 min):**
- **4 × 4 kipping HSPU**
  - Set-up: kick up to wall, active shoulders pressing the floor away, heels lightly touching wall, gaze between hands slightly forward
  - Hip kip to initiate: hips dip, then drive up as the press fires; each rep requires a full lockout — arms completely extended at the top; if lockout fails on any rep of a set, stop that set and add a 5 s negative to close
  - Rest 2:30 between sets — full recovery to maintain quality
  - Cap total wall time strictly at 8 min; if a set takes longer than expected, reduce to 3 reps rather than extending
  - Note: up from 4×3 in W3 — the extra rep per set is the W4 progression; prioritise lockout quality over hitting all 4 if fatigue compromises the press

**Core (5 min):**
