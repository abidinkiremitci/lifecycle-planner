## Running Session — 6 km Consolidate Tempo

### Warm-up (5 min)
- 3 min easy walk/jog (RPE 3)
- Leg swings (front-to-back and lateral) × 10/side
- Ankle circles × 10/side
- Calf raises × 15
- High knees × 20 (easy, in place)

### Main Run (30–35 min)

- **Option A — Continuous tempo: 6 km @ ~5:15/km target pace**
  - First 1 km: easy @ ~5:45/km — let the legs find their rhythm after Friday's squat session
  - km 2–5: hold tempo @ 5:10–5:20/km — strong but controlled; RPE 7, can speak short phrases but not hold a full conversation
  - Final 1 km: push to 5:00–5:10/km if legs allow
  - Target total time: ~31–32 min
- **Option B — Intervals: 4 × 1 km @ ~5:00/km**
  - Start with 1 km easy @ 5:45/km before the intervals
  - Rest 90 s easy walk between interval reps
  - RPE per interval: 8 — controlled hard effort; aim for even splits across all 4 reps; do not go sub-5:00 on rep 1
  - Target: all 4 reps at 5:00–5:10/km
- **Indoor alternative:** 30 min steady effort on rower or bike at RPE 6–7 (Zone 3)

### Cool-down (5 min)
- 3 min easy walk
- Wall calf stretch 2 × 45 s/side
- Seated single-leg hamstring fold 45 s/side
- Kneeling hip flexor stretch 45 s/side
