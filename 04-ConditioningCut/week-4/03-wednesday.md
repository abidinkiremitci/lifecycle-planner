## Strength (20 min)

- **Deadlift 4 × 4 @ ~80% (~135 kg)**, rest 2:30
  - Cues: hips back and down before the pull, neutral spine throughout, take the slack out of the bar before driving, squeeze glutes at lockout — every rep a reset on the floor
  - Note: +5 kg from Week 3's 130 kg — this is the final heavy deadlift of the cycle; form is the non-negotiable; if rep 4 requires back rounding, lower to 132.5 kg and log it
- **Accessory — BB bent-over row: 3 × 12 @ 72.5 kg**, rest 90 s
  - Cues: hip-hinge start at ~45° torso angle, pull bar to lower sternum with elbows driving back and wide, squeeze upper back at the top, controlled lower — do not bounce
  - Note: +2.5 kg from W3 at 70 kg; reps 10–12 should require focus without breaking form; stop the set if the lower back rounds to compensate

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 10–13 rounds)

- **16 box step-ups** (24" / 60 cm box, alternating legs) — sole cardio modality
- **8 DB thrusters** @ 22.5 kg/hand (scale: 15 kg/hand if lockout fails beyond round 5)
- **12 weighted sit-ups** (8 kg plate held at chest)

**Strategy:** Box step-ups at a brisk, steady pace — aim for ~2–2.5 s per rep (16 reps ≈ 35–40 s); keep breathing nasal through the step-ups if possible. DB thrusters unbroken for the first 6 rounds — 22.5 kg/hand is heavy for a long AMRAP; if the press lockout drifts before round 5, scale to 15 kg immediately rather than grinding through poor reps. Weighted sit-ups unbroken throughout — feet anchored, plate stays at chest on the way up, touch the floor on the way down; keep the movement controlled, not ballistic.

**Context:** Three movements to keep complexity low and allow consistent intensity — the consolidate week is about output, not novelty. The goal is the same effort level from round 1 through round 12+.

## Skill / Core (15 min)

**Pressing — seated DB overhead press + pike push-ups, no wall (10 min):**
- **Seated DB overhead press: 4 × 10 @ 15 kg/hand**, rest 75 s
  - Cues: sit tall, brace core, neutral wrist, press directly overhead — elbows slightly in front at the start position; do not let the head jut forward at lockout; stop the set if the shoulder shrugs to compensate
  - Note: up from 4×10 @ 12.5 kg/hand in W3 — this is the peak DB press load of the cycle; last 2 reps of each set should require focus but form holds
- **Pike push-ups from floor: 4 × 12**, 2 s eccentric lower
  - Set-up: hips high (inverted V), hands shoulder-width, lower crown toward floor between hands, explosive press back to top
  - Note: up from 4×10 in W3 — the extra 2 reps are the W4 volume progression for pike push-ups; maintain the 2 s eccentric throughout
