## Strength (20 min)

- **Back Squat 4 × 4 @ ~82% (~105 kg)**, rest 2:30
  - Cues: big breath and brace before descent, bar sits on rear delts (not neck), elbows driving down and in under the bar, knees tracking toes all the way through, powerful hip drive out of the hole — do not let the chest drop
  - Note: +5 kg from Week 3's 100 kg — this is the heaviest back squat of the cycle; if rep 4 on set 3 requires a serious grind, lower to 102.5 kg for set 4 and prioritise the 4 reps over the load
- **Accessory — chin-ups: 3 × max-reps BW**, rest 2:00

  - Note: W3 achieved 4×6; aim for 3 max-rep sets — this is the final pulling test of the cycle; record each set separately; if set 3 drops below 5, add a ring row finisher: 2 × 8 strict ring rows

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 8–10 rounds)

- **25 double unders** (alt: 60 single unders) — sole cardio modality
- **7 hang power snatches** @ 42.5 kg
- **10 ring dips**

**Strategy:** Double unders at a relaxed, rhythmic pace — 25 reps should take ~20–25 s; smooth rhythm, do not sprint the rope. Hang power snatches as fast singles — pull from the hang, shrug tall, punch overhead to a locked-out catch; 42.5 kg should feel fast and technical; if the bar drifts forward on the catch or the elbow bends, lower to 40 kg immediately. Ring dips unbroken for the first 5 rounds (rings slightly turned out at the bottom, full lockout at the top); split 6+4 when the push-out starts to slow; if ring dips are not available, substitute 10 bar dips or 12 push-up-to-downward-dog.

**Loading note:** Snatches after a heavy back squat session — prioritise a fast, vertical bar path over load; this is a skill-conditioning finish to the cycle, not a strength test.

**Alt (no rope):** Replace 25 DU with 10 box jump overs (24").

- 5 rounds, rest 60 s between rounds:

  - 8 pike push-ups from floor (hips high, hands shoulder-width, lower crown to floor)

- Total volume: 40 ring push-ups + 40 pike push-ups + 50 DB press reps
- Note: this is the highest single-session pressing volume of the cycle; if rounds 1–2 feel unsustainably taxing, reduce ring push-ups to 6 reps per round — do not shorten the 5 rounds; completing all 5 rounds is the priority
