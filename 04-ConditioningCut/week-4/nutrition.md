# Week 4 Nutrition — 04-ConditioningCut (2026-07-21 to 2026-07-27)

## Weekly Focus

Week 4 is the **Consolidate** phase — the final week of the 04-ConditioningCut cycle. The goal is to sustain peak output across all five sessions while holding the flat 2500 kcal deficit. This is not the week to reduce intake: reward four weeks of consistent training with full macro hits every day. Barbell loads are at their heaviest (front squat ~85 kg, deadlift ~135 kg, back squat ~105 kg), weighted pull-ups reach their peak load (+7.5 kg), and the Friday HSPU volume cluster accumulates the highest single-session pressing volume of the cycle. Wednesday's metcon is restored after being removed in W3.

The optional +30 g carbs pre-workout on Monday and Friday is worth activating this week given the increased barbell loads and pressing volume. Do not cut these from another meal — add them as a pre-workout boost only.

## Key Timing Reminders

- **Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri — 60–90 min before):** 400–500 kcal with 60–75 g carbs + 30–40 g protein, fat <15 g; 500 ml water on waking; activate the +30 g carbs option on Monday (85 kg front squat + +7.5 kg pull-ups) and Friday (105 kg back squat + HSPU volume cluster)
- **Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri — within 45 min):** 40–50 g protein + 60–70 g carbs; keep fat minimal in this window
- **Pre-row (Tue — 60 min before):** 400–500 kcal with 60–70 g carbs + 25–35 g protein; 3×2000 m intervals demand consistent glycogen — do not under-fuel this session
- **Pre-run (Sat or Sun — 60–90 min before):** 300–450 kcal with 50–65 g carbs + 20–30 g protein; both Option A (continuous tempo) and Option B (4×1 km intervals) require adequate glycogen

## Recovery & Deficit Notes

- **Wednesday metcon restored:** Total weekly metabolic demand is back to a full three-metcon FF week. Hold 2500 kcal — do not increase because the metcon returned; the body has adapted to the deficit over four weeks.
- **Final cycle benchmarks to track:**
  - Weighted pull-up load and reps (Mon: 4×5 @ BW+7.5 kg) — compare to W1 baseline (3×5 BW)
  - HSPU reps per set on Monday (4×4 kipping) — compare to W1 (negatives and holds)
  - Row splits (3×2000 m vs. W1's 5 km steady @ ≤2:35)
  - Run pace (6 km tempo @ ~5:15 vs. W1's easy 5 km @ ~6:00)
- **Performance decay check:** If two consecutive FF sessions feel flat despite hitting 265 g carbs, add +100–150 kcal via carbs immediately. Do not push through performance decay in the final week — it is a signal, not a character test.
- **Ankle monitoring:** Monday HSPU is 4×4 — cap total wall time at 8 min. Note any ankle irritation after Monday for Cycle 5 planning.
- **Scale tracking:** ~0.5–0.75 kg/week fat loss expected. If the final week shows no loss or a gain, hold course — a single week's fluctuation is noise. Four weeks of consistent data is what matters.
- **Hydration:** 3.5–4 L water daily baseline; add ~500 ml per training hour; electrolytes (sodium 500–700 mg/L + potassium) on all five training days this week.
- **End of cycle:** After Sunday, note total performance changes across four weeks before planning Cycle 5.
