## Main Row

### 3 × 2000 m Intervals
- **3 × 2000 m** @ ≤2:28/500 m per rep
- Rest: 3:00 easy row or walk between reps (rate ~16 spm)
- Stroke rate during intervals: 22–24 spm
- RPE per interval: 8 — sustained hard effort, controlled throughout

**Pacing structure:**
- Rep 1: settle at 2:26–2:28 — do not go out hot; the 2000 m piece demands conservative pacing; establish rhythm in the first 500 m
- Rep 2: hold 2:24–2:27 — consistency is the primary goal; even splits are better than a fast rep followed by a dying one
- Rep 3: push to 2:22–2:25 if legs and breathing allow — finish the cycle's final row session with a negative split on the last rep

**Technique reminders:**
- Drive phase: legs push first, hips open second, arms pull last; at 2000 m pace this sequence often degrades as fatigue builds — stay deliberate
- The 2000 m distance at ≤2:28/500 m is approximately 9:52 per rep; treat each as a controlled time trial, not a sprint
- Don't let stroke rate creep above 24 spm when chasing the split; power per stroke at 22 spm is more sustainable than sloppy pulls at 26 spm

**Context from W3:** W3 intervals (5×1000 m) averaged 2:19–2:21/500 m. The 3×2000 m pieces at ≤2:28 are a longer sustained effort — the target is conservative relative to W3 pace, but the 2000 m distance adds a different metabolic demand. Aim for consistent 2:24–2:26 splits rather than chasing the sub-2:20 pace from shorter intervals.
