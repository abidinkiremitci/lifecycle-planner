# Week 3 Nutrition — 04-ConditioningCut (2026-07-14 to 2026-07-20)

## Weekly Focus

Week 3 is the **Push Output** phase — the heaviest barbell loads of the cycle, peak accessory volume, and the introduction of VO2 rowing intervals (5×1000 m). However, **Wednesday's metcon has been removed** this week to support recovery, making the overall metabolic demand more moderate than a standard Push Output week. The flat 2500 kcal target continues unchanged. The priority this week is hitting the 265 g carb target consistently on Monday and Friday to fuel the two high-intensity FF sessions; Wednesday and Thursday can be treated as moderate loading days with normal intake. The optional +30 g carbs pre-workout on FF days is worth activating on Monday given the heavier front squat and weighted pull-up loads.

## Key Timing Reminders

- **Pre-workout (Mon / Fri — 60–90 min before session):** 400–500 kcal with 60–75 g carbs + 30–40 g protein, fat <15 g; 500 ml water on waking; activate the +30 g carbs option on Monday given weighted pull-ups and 4×4 front squat
- **Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri — within 45 min):** 40–50 g protein + 60–70 g carbs; keep fat minimal in this window
- **Pre-row (Tue — 60 min before):** 400–500 kcal with 60–70 g carbs + 25–35 g protein; intervals demand glycogen — do not under-fuel this session
- **Pre-run (Sun — 60–90 min before):** 300–450 kcal with 50–65 g carbs + 20–30 g protein; the embedded tempo block needs adequate glycogen — even a small meal is better than running fasted
- **Wednesday (accessory day):** No structural pre-workout fuelling change needed — normal meal timing around the session; the session is lower intensity than a standard metcon day

## Recovery & Deficit Notes

- **Wednesday metcon removed:** Total weekly metabolic load is reduced vs. a standard W3. Do not compensate by reducing calories — maintain 2500 kcal flat; the deficit is already aggressive enough.
- **Recovery signal tracking:** If Monday and Friday FF sessions feel significantly better than the past two weeks, the Wednesday load reduction is working. If fatigue persists after this week, consider keeping the no-metcon Wednesday structure for Week 4 as well.
- **Weighted pull-up note:** First week of +5 kg weighted pull-ups. Record exact load and reps per set on Monday. Horizontal row volume (Wednesday BB row 3×12 @ 70 kg) should feel challenging at reps 10–12 — note if it requires pausing mid-set.
- **HSPU note:** 4×3 kipping HSPU on Monday is the highest wall volume yet. Note any ankle irritation after the session — if present, reduce from 4 to 3 sets next week rather than adding more sets.
- **Rowing note:** 5×1000 m intervals replace the steady-state option for the first time. Fuel well the night before (hit the 265 g carb target on Monday) so Tuesday legs have glycogen available.
- **Adjustment rule:** If two consecutive FF sessions feel flat despite hitting 265 g carbs, raise to +30 g carbs on remaining training days and reassess at the end of the week.
- **Scale tracking:** ~0.5–0.75 kg/week fat loss expected. If loss exceeds 1 kg, add +100–150 kcal via carbs immediately. Weigh under the same conditions (morning, fasted, after using the bathroom).
- **Hydration:** 3.5–4 L water daily baseline; add ~500 ml per training hour; electrolytes (sodium 500–700 mg/L + potassium) on Mon, Wed, Fri (FF), Tue (row intervals), and Sun (run with tempo).
