## Strength (20 min)

- **Deadlift 4 × 4 @ ~78% (~130 kg)**, rest 2:30
  - Cues: hips back and down before the pull, neutral spine throughout, take the slack out of the bar slowly then drive the floor away, squeeze glutes at lockout — every rep a reset on the floor
  - Note: +10 kg from Week 2 (120 kg), reps drop from 5 to 4 — this is the heaviest deadlift of the cycle; if rep 4 requires back rounding, lower to 125 kg; form is the non-negotiable here
- **Accessory — BB bent-over row: 3 × 12 @ 70 kg**, rest 90 s
  - Cues: hip-hinge start at ~45° torso angle, pull bar to lower sternum with elbows driving back and wide, squeeze the upper back at the top, controlled lower — do not bounce
  - Note: up from DB rows in previous weeks — BB row allows heavier horizontal pull; 70 kg should challenge the last 3 reps of each set without breaking form

## Accessory Block — Posterior Chain (replaces metcon — ~28 min)

*Controlled loading, no metabolic rush. Rest as needed between exercises; this is a strength quality block, not conditioning.*

**A — Bulgarian Split Squat: 3 × 8/side @ 2 × 12 kg DBs**, rest 90 s between sides
- Set-up: rear foot elevated on bench or box at roughly knee height, front foot far enough forward that the shin stays vertical at the bottom
- Cues: lower straight down under control (2 s eccentric), front knee tracks over the toes, keep the torso upright — do not lean forward onto the front leg; drive through the front heel to stand
- Note: this deliberately avoids any lower back loading after the heavy deadlift — if 12 kg/hand feels too easy, move to 14 kg next set; if balance is the limiting factor, drop DBs to 8 kg and focus on the depth and control

**B — Single-leg KB Deadlift: 3 × 8/side @ 20 kg**, rest 90 s between sides
- Cues: standing foot flat, slight bend in the working knee, hinge by driving the hip back while the trailing leg lifts — opposite arm holds the KB, trailing leg stays in line with the torso; keep hips square throughout
- Note: slow, controlled movement — this is balance + unilateral posterior chain; if balance is limiting, lightly touch the trail foot to the floor between reps rather than wobbling through

**C — KB Glute Bridge: 3 × 15 @ 24 kg KB across hips**, rest 90 s
- Cues: feet flat, knees at 90°, drive through the heels, squeeze glutes hard at the top for 1 s — do not hyperextend the lower back; KB sits just below the hip bones on a folded towel for comfort
- Note: this is low-load posterior activation — rest period allows full recovery; the goal is glute isolation, not fatigue accumulation

## Skill / Core (18 min — expanded)

**Pressing — DB overhead + pike push-ups, no wall (10 min):**
- **Seated DB overhead press: 4 × 10 @ 14 kg/hand**, rest 75 s
  - Cues: sit tall, brace core, neutral wrist, press directly overhead — do not let the head jut forward at the lockout; elbows slightly in front of the bar at the start position
  - Note: up from 3×10 @ 12 kg/hand in W2 — both a load and a set increase; last 2 reps of each set should require focus but not a breakdown in form; stop the set if the shoulder shrugs to compensate
- **Pike push-ups from floor: 4 × 10**, 2 s eccentric lower
  - Set-up: hips high (inverted V), hands shoulder-width, lower crown toward floor between hands, explosive press back to top
  - Note: up from 4×8 in W2; the extra 2 reps per set are the W3 progression — prioritise full ROM over speed
