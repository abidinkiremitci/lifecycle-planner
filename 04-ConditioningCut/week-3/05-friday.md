## Strength (20 min)

- **Back Squat 4 × 4 @ ~78% (~100 kg)**, rest 2:30
  - Cues: big breath and brace before descent, bar sits on rear delts (not neck), elbows driving down and in under the bar, knees tracking toes all the way through, powerful hip drive out of the hole — do not morning-star the rep
  - Note: +5 kg from Week 2 (95 kg), reps drop from 5 to 4 — each set should be heavy but controlled; if the chest collapses on rep 4, lower to 97.5 kg
- **Accessory — chin-ups: 4 × 6 BW**, rest 90 s
  - Cues: supinated grip (palms toward face), dead hang start, scapula packed before pulling, chin to the bar or above, controlled 2 s lower — no kip, no swing
  - Note: up from 3×6 + ring row bonus in W2; 4 full sets of chin-ups is the W3 volume target — log exact reps per set; if set 4 drops below 5, add a ring row finisher set to complete the volume

## Metcon (AMRAP 23 — target 7–9 rounds)

- **8 burpee bar hop** — sole cardio modality (lie down → push up → jump/step laterally over the barbell → that is 1 rep; bar height is ~20 cm off the floor)
- **8 overhead squats** @ 35 kg
- **12 strict push-ups**
- **15 hollow rocks**

**Strategy:** Burpee bar hops at a consistent, methodical pace — breathe at the top of each rep before the jump; the lateral hop keeps you in the 5 m space. Overhead squats unbroken all rounds — 35 kg is intentionally light; the focus is on locked-out arms, active shoulder press against the bar at the top, and a stable catch at the bottom; break 4+4 only if the overhead position starts to drift. Strict push-ups in two sets from round 4 onward if shoulders accumulate fatigue: 7+5. Hollow rocks unbroken throughout — full dish position, lower back maintains contact with the floor; if it arches, stop the set and reset.

**Loading note:** If the overhead position in the OHS feels unstable on any rep (elbows bending, bar drifting forward), drop to 30 kg immediately — do not compromise the catch position in a fatigued state.
