## Main Row

### 5 × 1000 m Intervals
- **5 × 1000 m** @ ≤2:26/500 m per rep
- Rest: 2:30 easy row or walk between reps (rate ~16 spm)
- Stroke rate during intervals: 22–24 spm
- RPE per interval: 7–8 — controlled hard effort; each rep should feel the same or slightly stronger

**Pacing structure:**
- Rep 1: settle at 2:24–2:26 — do not go out hot; establish rhythm first
- Reps 2–4: hold exactly 2:22–2:26 — consistency is the goal, not speed; even splits are better than a fast first rep
- Rep 5: push to 2:20–2:22 if breathing and form allow — finish the set strong, not desperate

**Technique reminders:**
- Drive phase: legs push first, hips open second, arms pull last — sequence matters more as fatigue builds
- Recovery phase: arms out first, hips rock forward, legs fold — catch tall with a strong back angle
- Don't let the stroke rate creep above 24 spm when chasing the split — a clean 22 spm drives more power than sloppy 26 spm

**Note:** W2 row performance (6,000 m at 2:05 avg) suggests strong capacity. The 2:26 target is conservative — aim to negative split rep 5 to 2:20 if the first four feel controlled.
