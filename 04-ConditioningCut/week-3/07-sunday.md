## Warm-up (5 min)

- Leg swings (front-to-back + lateral) × 10/side

---

## Main Run (~36 min)

**6 km total — 2 km tempo embedded mid-run**

- **Km 1–2: Easy @ ~5:45–6:00/km** (Zone 2, nasal breathing, RPE 5; warm the legs up gradually — do not start at tempo pace)
- **Km 3–4: Tempo @ ~5:00–5:10/km** (RPE 7–8, controlled hard effort; mouth breathing is fine; maintain posture and cadence — target 170–175 spm)
- **Km 5–6: Easy @ ~5:45–6:00/km** (RPE 5–6; allow the body to settle back to Zone 2 pace; this doubles as a running cool-down)

**Strategy:** The tempo block should feel like a "comfortably hard" pace — not a sprint. If at km 3 the legs still feel heavy from the week, shift the tempo block to km 4–5 and keep the full warm-up easy. Do not skip the tempo — this is the VO2 stimulus for the week.

**Note — W2 recovery context:** Sunday run was skipped in Week 2 due to poor recovery. The reduced Wednesday metcon this week should leave more in the tank for this session. If at the warm-up the legs still feel unusually heavy, keep the full 6 km at easy pace (drop the tempo) and log the reason — that is a valid adaptation signal.

**Indoor alternative (treadmill):** Same structure — set incline to 0.5–1% to simulate road effort.
