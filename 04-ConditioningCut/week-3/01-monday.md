## Strength (20 min)

- **Front Squat 4 × 4 @ ~78% (~80 kg)**, rest 2:30
  - Cues: elbows high and forward before descent, brace hard before the hole, drive knees out aggressively, lead with elbows on the way up — do not let the chest drop
  - Note: +5 kg from Week 2, reps drop from 5 to 4 — each set should feel heavy but not a max effort; if rep 4 feels like a true grind, lower to 77.5 kg and log it
- **Accessory — weighted strict pull-ups: 4 × 5 @ BW + 5 kg**, rest 2:00
  - Cues: dead hang start, full scapular depression before pulling, chin clearly over bar, controlled lower — no kip; keep the added weight stable (KB between ankles or belt)
  - Note: first weighted pull-up sets this cycle; if reps 4–5 feel like a struggle, drop load to +2.5 kg and preserve the 4×5 volume

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 7–9 rounds)

- **100 single unders** 
- **7 power cleans** @ 60 kg
- **12 strict push-ups**
- **14 hanging knee raises**

**Strategy:** Double unders at a relaxed, controlled rhythm — 50 reps should take ~30–35 s; never sprint the rope, rhythm is the key. Power cleans as fast singles or 3+2+2 splits — reset at the hip between reps, no touch-and-go (the reset keeps mechanics consistent across rounds). Strict push-ups unbroken through rounds 1–5 (save shoulders for the work ahead), then 7+5 if needed. Hanging knee raises unbroken throughout — pull knees above the hip crease, pause briefly at the top, do not let the body swing.

**Loading note:** 60 kg on the power clean should feel fast at the turnover — if the bar is crashing onto the shoulders or elbows are slow to fire under, drop to 57.5 kg. This is the push-output week; protect the mechanics first, then add weight next cycle.

## Skill / Core (13 min)

**HSPU — direct wall work (8 min):**
- **4 × 3 kipping HSPU**
  - Set-up: kick up to wall, active shoulders pressing the floor away, heels lightly touching wall, eyes between hands at a point slightly forward
  - Use hip kip to initiate: hips dip slightly, then drive up as the press fires; control the lower but don't slow the kip initiation
  - Each rep must be a full lockout — arms completely extended at the top; if lockout fails on any rep of a set, stop that set there and add a 5 s negative to close
  - Rest 2:30 between sets — full recovery between sets to maintain quality
  - Cap total wall time here strictly at 8 min; if a set takes longer than expected, reduce to 2 reps rather than exceeding the time cap

**Core (5 min):**
- 3 rounds:
  - 12 dead bugs/side (full extension: opposite arm/leg, lower back glued to floor throughout)
  - 35 s hollow hold (up from 30 s in W2)
