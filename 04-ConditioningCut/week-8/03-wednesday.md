## Strength (20 min)

- **Deadlift 4 × 3 @ ~75% (~125 kg)**, rest 2:30
  - Cues: hips back and down before the pull, neutral spine throughout — do not round the lower back at any point; take the slack out of the bar before driving; squeeze glutes at lockout
  - Note: Down from 140 kg peak in W5–6; this is a movement retest after back injury — stop the set and reduce load immediately if any back pain appears; a clean triple at 120 kg beats a compromised rep at 125 kg; log back pain rep by rep if needed
  - **Back injury fallback:** If >2/10 back pain on warm-up rep 1 → drop to 4 × 5 KB Romanian deadlift @ 24 kg (supported hinge, safer pattern)
- **Accessory — DB single-arm row 3 × 12/side @ 22.5 kg** (knee + hand on bench — supported), rest 60 s between sides
  - Using supported DB row this week instead of BB bent-over row — reduces free lumbar loading during the return week
  - Cues: pull elbow to hip, squeeze at the top, 2 s controlled lower — no torso rotation
