- **Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri):** 300–400 kcal with 50–60 g carbs + 30–40 g protein, fat <10 g, 60–90 min before session; 500 ml water on waking
- **Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri):** 40–50 g protein + 50–60 g carbs within 45 min
- **Pre-row (Tue):** 300–400 kcal with 40–55 g carbs + 25–35 g protein; 60 min before
- **Pre-run (Sat):** 300–400 kcal with 50–60 g carbs + 20–30 g protein; 60–90 min before

## Recovery & Cycle-Close Notes

- **Back fuelling:** Returning to loaded barbell work increases metabolic demand vs Week 7. If energy is clearly low on Monday, add +30 g carbs pre-workout on training days for the remainder of the week.
- **Protein stays at 185 g every day:** Back is still healing even while lifting — do not reduce protein on rest days.
- **Scale tracking:** Last full week of the cycle. Compare weight to Week 1 for 8-week total. Expected cycle-total fat loss at these targets: ~3–5 kg depending on adherence across the peak weeks.
- **Adjustment rule:** If any session causes back pain above 3/10 and needs to be scaled back, raise that day's calories to 2400 kcal (add carbs) to compensate for any cortisol spike from pain stress.
- **Cycle transition:** After Sunday, allow 3–5 days at maintenance (~2700–2800 kcal) before starting Cycle 05 to let back recovery consolidate and hormonal baselines normalise.
