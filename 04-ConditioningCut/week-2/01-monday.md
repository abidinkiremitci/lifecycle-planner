## Strength (15–20 min)

- **Front Squat 4 × 5 @ ~72% (~75 kg)**, rest 2:00
  - Cues: elbows high and forward, upright torso, full-foot pressure, drive knees out, aggressive extension from the hole
  - Note: +5 kg from Week 1 — first 3 reps should feel controlled at depth; reps 4–5 can be a moderate challenge; do not grind rep 5
- **Accessory — strict pull-ups: 3 × 6 BW**, rest 90 s
  - Cues: dead hang start, full scapular depression before pulling, chin clearly over bar, controlled lower — no kip
  - Note: up from 3×5 in Week 1; aim for all three sets unbroken; log reps per set

## Metcon (AMRAP 20 — target 5–6 rounds)

- **40 double unders** — sole cardio modality
  - Alt (no DU): 80 single unders
- **8 thrusters** @ 37.5 kg
- **10 ring dips**
- **12 hanging knee raises**

**Strategy:** Double unders at a steady, relaxed rhythm — aim to finish 40 in ~25–30 s; take a short breath before moving to thrusters. Thrusters unbroken through rounds 1–4 (hip-drive initiated — let the leg pop carry the bar overhead, not the shoulders); 5+3 from round 5 if shoulders fatigue. Ring dips unbroken rounds 1–3 (turn rings out at the bottom, rotate in at the top for shoulder safety), then 6+4. Hanging knee raises unbroken throughout — controlled, pull knees above the hip crease at the top.

**Loading note:** 37.5 kg on the thruster should feel fast and connected — drop to 35 kg if the overhead catch feels unstable or the elbows are drifting forward.

## Skill / Core (13 min)

**HSPU — direct wall work (8 min):**
- 3 × 3 kipping HSPU
  - Set-up: kick up to wall, active shoulders (push floor away), eyes between hands, heels lightly on wall
  - Use leg drive and hip kip to initiate the press; control the lower but don't slow the kip
  - If rep 3 of any set feels rough, cap at 2 and add one 5 s negative to close the set — do not push to failure
  - Rest 2 min between sets; cap total wall time here, no extra sets
- Note: progressing from W1 controlled negatives to full kipping reps; quality over volume

**Core (5 min):**
- 3 rounds:
  - 10 dead bugs/side (full extension, lower back stays flat throughout)
  - 30 s hollow hold (up from 25 s in W1)
