# Week 2 Nutrition — 04-ConditioningCut (2026-07-07 to 2026-07-13)

## Weekly Focus

Week 2 is the **Volume Build** phase — accessory rep counts are rising across all three FF sessions, metcons are pushing toward 20–22 min quality output, and the rowing session steps up to 6 km or 4×1000 m intervals. The flat 2500 kcal target continues the deficit. Hitting the 265 g carb target consistently is especially important this week: higher reps on pull-ups, DB rows, chin-ups, and pressing will draw more on glycogen than last week's baseline sets. Under-eating carbs this week is the most common cause of stalled accessory rep progressions — protein is fixed at 185 g as the non-negotiable floor.

## Key Timing Reminders

- **Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri — 60–90 min before session):** 400–500 kcal with 60–75 g carbs + 30–40 g protein, fat <15 g; 500 ml water on waking
- **Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri — within 45 min):** 40–50 g protein + 60–70 g carbs; keep fat minimal in this window
- **Pre-row (Tue — 60 min before):** 400–500 kcal with 60–70 g carbs + 25–35 g protein; adjust timing around work schedule
- **Pre-run (Sun — 60–90 min before):** 300–450 kcal with 50–65 g carbs + 20–30 g protein; even a small meal is better than running fasted at this deficit
- **Bike commute (Thu):** No structured pre-fuelling needed — normal meal timing around commute

## Recovery & Deficit Notes

- **Volume Build signal:** If accessories (pull-ups Mon, DB rows Wed, chin-ups Fri) feel significantly harder than Week 1 despite consistent carbs, activate the +30 g carbs pre-workout option on the next FF session and monitor for one session before increasing further.
- **Ankle monitoring:** Wall contact only on Monday, capped at 8 min total. Wednesday and Friday have zero wall exposure by design. If Monday's ankles are irritated after kipping HSPU, note it and reduce sets from 3 to 2 — do not push through pain.
- **Pull-up tracking:** Record exact reps per set for both pull-ups (Monday) and chin-ups (Friday). Horizontal row load (Wednesday) target is 24 kg × 3 × 12 — note if the last 2 reps per set required a hip rotation cheat; if so, drop to 22.5 kg and rebuild.
- **Rowing note:** Week 1 row was completed well under the 2:35 prescribed split (5 km at 2:08 avg). Option B intervals (4×1000 m @ ≤2:26/500 m) are recommended for Tuesday if energy is solid coming off Monday's session; take Option A (6 km steady) if legs feel heavy.
- **HSPU note:** Monday's 3×3 kipping reps is the first kipping session this cycle. If the kip feels unstable on any set, cap at 2 reps and add a 5 s negative — do not push through shaky reps.
- **Adjustment rule:** If two consecutive FF sessions feel flat despite hitting 265 g carbs, raise to +30 g carbs on remaining training days that week and reassess the following week.
- **Scale tracking:** ~0.5–0.75 kg/week fat loss expected at 2500 kcal. Weigh consistently (same time, same conditions). If loss exceeds 1 kg this week, add +100–150 kcal via carbs immediately.
- **Hydration:** 3.5–4 L water daily baseline; add ~500 ml per training hour; electrolytes (sodium 500–700 mg/L + potassium) on Mon/Wed/Fri, Tue (row session), and Sun (run).
