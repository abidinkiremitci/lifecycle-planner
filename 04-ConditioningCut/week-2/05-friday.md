## Strength (15–20 min)

- **Back Squat 4 × 5 @ ~72% (~95 kg)**, rest 2:00
  - Cues: big breath and brace before descent, bar on rear delts or traps (preference), elbows pointing down under the bar, knees tracking toes through full range, powerful drive out of the hole
  - Note: up from ~90 kg in Week 1 — a moderate step; stay crisp and controlled; do not let the knees cave or chest collapse on the drive up
- **Accessory — chin-ups 3 × 6 BW + ring rows 1 × max-reps**, rest 90 s
  - Chin-up cues: supinated grip, dead hang start, pull chin to bar, controlled lower — no kip
  - Ring row cues: lean back to ~45°, pull chest to hands, full extension at the bottom; adjust foot position to scale difficulty
  - Note: up from 3×5 chin-ups in Week 1; the ring row set is a bonus volume set — aim for 10+ reps; log exact reps

## Metcon (AMRAP 20 — target 4–5 rounds)

- **6 devil press** @ 2 × 15 kg DBs — sole cardio modality
- **8 overhead squats** @ 30 kg
- **12 KB sumo deadlift high pulls (SDHP)** @ 24 kg
- **10 V-ups**

**Strategy:** Devil press at a deliberate pace — breathe once at the top of each rep before going back down. Overhead squats unbroken all rounds (30 kg is intentionally light — focus on locked-out arms, active shoulders pressing the bar up, and a stable catch at the bottom; break 4+4 only if the lockout starts collapsing). KB SDHP unbroken all rounds — drive elbows high, the bell should reach chest height with elbows above the handle at the top. V-ups unbroken — full extension on the way down, controlled crunch to touch toes.

**Loading note:** If 15 kg per hand on devil press makes the hip snap feel sluggish, drop to 12.5 kg. For OHS, 30 kg is light by design — if the overhead position feels shaky on any rep, drop to 25 kg and focus on the catch.

- **Seated DB press: 3 × 8 @ 12 kg/hand**, rest 60 s
