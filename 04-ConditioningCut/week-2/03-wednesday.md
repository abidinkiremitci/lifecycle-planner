## Strength (15–20 min)

- **Deadlift 4 × 5 @ ~72% (~120 kg)**, rest 2:00
  - Cues: hips back before the pull, neutral spine throughout, drive the floor away rather than pulling the bar, squeeze glutes hard at lockout
  - Note: +10 kg from Week 1; same mechanics focus — reset on the floor between reps (no touch-and-go), brace hard before each rep; the step-up should feel challenging at reps 4–5 but never a form compromise
- **Accessory — DB single-arm row: 3 × 12/side @ 24 kg**, rest 60 s between sides
  - Cues: flat back, elbow drives back and up, full range from hanging shoulder to hip pocket, 1 s hold at top
  - Note: up from 3×10 @ 22 kg in Week 1; last 2 reps of each side should be controlled — no hip rotation cheat to finish the set

## Metcon (AMRAP 21 — target 4–5 rounds)

- **12 cal row** — sole cardio modality
- **6 hang squat cleans** @ 50 kg
- **12 DB push press** @ 2 × 15 kg
- **14 hollow rocks**

**Strategy:** Row at a hard push (~1:55–2:00/500 m pace) to clear 12 cals in ~25–30 s — powerful strokes, not spinning. Hang squat cleans unbroken rounds 1–3 (pull elbows up fast and sit into the catch; the front rack should land stable before standing); 3+3 from round 4 if the catch is getting sloppy. DB push press unbroken all rounds (use the dip-drive — 15 kg should move quickly; elbows slightly in front of the bar at the start). Hollow rocks unbroken — maintain the dish position throughout; stop the set early if the lower back loses contact with the floor rather than struggling through broken reps.

**Loading note:** 50 kg on the hang squat clean is the focus movement this session — drop to 47.5 kg if the turnover under the bar is slow or the front rack catch feels unstable.

- **Seated DB overhead press: 3 × 10 @ 12 kg/hand**, rest 60 s
