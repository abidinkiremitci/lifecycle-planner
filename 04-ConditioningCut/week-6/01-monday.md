## Strength (20 min)

- **Front Squat 5 × 3 @ ~85% (~87.5 kg)**, rest 2:30
  - Cues: elbows high and forward before descent, brace hard before the hole, knees out aggressively, lead with elbows on the drive up — do not let the chest drop forward
  - Note: intensity steps up to ~85% with fewer reps per set (5×3 vs W4's 4×4 @ 85 kg) — the lower rep count allows the heavier load while preserving quality; each triple should feel controlled and powerful; if set 5 rep 3 is a real grind, log it as the cycle-end front squat benchmark; do not attempt a 4th rep

  - Note: +2.5 kg from Week 4's +7.5 kg; this is the peak weighted pull-up load of the cycle; if rep 5 fails on sets 3–4, complete remaining reps at +7.5 kg and record both loads; completing 4 full sets matters more than the extra load

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 9–12 rounds)

- **10 burpees over barbell** — sole cardio modality
- **8 push jerks** @ 52.5 kg
- **12 KB goblet squats** @ 24 kg

**Strategy:** Burpees over barbell at a steady rhythmic pace (~3–4 s per rep, 10 reps ≈ 35–40 s); step or jump over — do not sprint and gas the legs early. Push jerks: aggressive hip drive and punch overhead, arms fully locked out before receiving the bar back; 52.5 kg should feel powerful in rounds 1–5 and demanding by round 9. Goblet squats unbroken throughout — hold the KB by the horns at chest height, elbows inside the knees, full depth; use the descent as a brief recovery between jerks and the next set of burpees.

**Loading note:** If the push jerk catch position feels unstable (bar drifting forward or elbows bending under load) from round 5, drop to 50 kg and preserve the mechanics.

**Alt (no barbell for burpees):** Replace burpees over barbell with 10 lateral burpees (jump laterally over a KB or chalk line).
