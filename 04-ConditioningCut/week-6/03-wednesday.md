## Posterior Chain Accessory Block (25–30 min)

*Desk-work compensation focus: upper back, mid-back, glutes, and hamstrings. Controlled tempo throughout — this is not conditioning; treat every rep as deliberate muscle work.*

### A. Face Pulls — 4 × 15 (rest 60 s)
- Anchor a resistance band at upper-chest height
- Pull toward the face with elbows flared high (elbows stay above wrists throughout), externally rotate at the end of the pull so hands finish behind ears
- 2 s hold at peak contraction, 2 s return
- Cues: retract the scapula before pulling, do not shrug; this directly counters the rounded-shoulder posture of desk work

### B. Single-Leg KB Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 10/side @ 24 kg (rest 75 s)
- Stand on one leg, hinge at the hip with a soft knee, lower the KB toward the floor while the free leg extends behind; return to standing by driving the hip forward
- Cues: keep the spine neutral and the planted foot gripping the floor; the free-leg extension is deliberate hip-hinge loading, not a balance trick; feel the hamstring load at the bottom
- Note: unilateral loading exposes left-right asymmetries from prolonged sitting — note which side feels weaker

### C. Prone DB Y-T-W Raises — 3 × 10 reps of each letter (rest 60 s between letters; no rest between Y, T, and W)
- Lie face-down on the floor or bench, arms start at sides
- Y: raise arms to a Y shape (thumb up), squeeze mid-traps and lower traps at the top — 2 s hold
- T: arms out to the sides (T shape), retract scapula and lift — 2 s hold
- W: elbows bent ~90°, pull them toward the hips and back (W shape) — 2 s hold
- Load: 2–5 kg DBs or no weight if the movement is new; this is mid-trap and rotator cuff activation, not a strength lift
- Cues: no neck strain — keep the head neutral (eyes to floor); each letter is a separate movement, not a flowing sequence

### D. Glute Bridge Hold — 3 × 45 s (rest 60 s)
- Lie on back, feet flat, drive hips to full extension and hold
- At the top: squeeze glutes hard, neutral lower back (do not hyperextend), knees tracking over toes
- Add a 20 kg plate on the hips if the hold feels easy
- Cues: the goal is sustained posterior chain activation, not just holding a position; actively press through the heels and squeeze the glutes throughout

### E. Banded Pull-Aparts — 3 × 20 (rest 45 s)
- Hold a resistance band at shoulder width, arms straight out in front
- Pull the band apart to chest level, squeezing the rear delts and mid-traps at end range
- 1 s hold at full pull, controlled return
- Cues: keep the arms straight (slight elbow bend is fine); do not shrug; this is rear delt and upper-back activation for posture correction
