## Warm-up (10 min)

  - 10 glute bridges (slow squeeze — activates glutes and posterior chain safely)
  - 10 scapula push-ups (protraction–retraction, arms straight, no sag)
  - 10 band pull-aparts
  - 8 ring rows (easy, chest-to-rings, warmup tempo)
  - 10 hip 90/90 switches/side (lying, controlled)
- Shoulder prep: 10 Cuban press @ 5 kg/hand (external rotation warm-up before pressing)

---

## Strength (15 min)

- **DB single-arm row 3 × 12/side @ 22.5 kg**, rest 60 s between sides
  - Set-up: same-side knee and hand on bench (fully supported), back parallel to floor — bench support eliminates free lumbar loading entirely
  - Cues: pull elbow to hip (not flared to shoulder), squeeze rhomboid at the top, 2 s controlled lower — no torso rotation, no jerking
  - Note: 22.5 kg is the heaviest DB available; if form breaks before rep 12, stop the set and log reps completed — do not compromise the back for the count
- **DB bench press 3 × 12 @ 22.5 kg/hand**, rest 90 s
  - Cues: retract and depress shoulder blades before each set, press to full lockout, 2 s lower; feet flat on floor, mild natural arch only — no exaggerated low-back arch
  - Note: Bench press is the safest pressing variation for back injury; the spine is fully supported; focus on the pressing mechanics and chest squeeze at lockout

---

## Metcon (3 rounds for time — target 15–18 min)

- **16 box step-ups** (8/side, ~45 cm box, unloaded) — sole cardio modality; controlled step, full foot contact
- **12 ring rows** (rings at hip height, body horizontal — walk feet closer to rings to make easier; further away to make harder)
- **12 dips** (dip bar — full range, elbows to ~90°; scale to 8 reps if shoulders fatigue)
- **8 DB push press @ 15 kg/hand** (light dip-and-drive; at this load, lumbar involvement is minimal — stop if low back aches during the dip)

**Strategy:** Rest 90 s between rounds; move steadily between movements within each round. Push press should be unbroken all 3 rounds at 15 kg. Dips are the most likely limiting factor — break to sets of 6 in round 2 if needed. Ring rows: walk feet 5–10 cm forward each round to progressively challenge. RPE 7–8.
