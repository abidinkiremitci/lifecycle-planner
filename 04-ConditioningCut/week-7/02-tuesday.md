## Option A — Easy Row (back pain 0–3)

### Warm-up (5 min)
- 5 min easy row @ rate 16–18 spm, split >3:00/500 m — loosening only
- 10 slow hip circles each direction (standing off rower)
- 10 band pull-aparts

### Main Row (20 min)
- 20 min continuous @ ≤2:45/500 m, stroke rate 20–22 spm
- Focus: rowing technique — arms-body-legs sequence on the drive; legs-body-arms on the recovery; slow the slide, use the legs
- RPE 4–5 — fully conversational effort; if low back tightens at any point, stop immediately and switch to Option B mobility
- Intentionally low stroke rate to keep force per stroke light and avoid lumbar strain on the drive

### Cool-down (5 min)
- 5 min easy row @ rate 16 spm
- Seated forward fold off the rower seat 60 s (feet on floor, hinge from hips — stop before pain)
- Hip flexor stretch 45 s/side

## Option B — Back Mobility Session (back pain 4–6)

### Mobility Session (30 min)

1. **Cat-cow** — 3 min continuous, slow rhythm (pain-free range only; do not push into the painful arc)
2. **Supine knees-to-chest hold** — 2 × 60 s (pull both knees in gently; gentle lumbar decompression)
3. **Supine 90/90 hip rotation** — 10/side × 3 sets (feet on floor, knees drop slowly to one side; do not force)
4. **Dead bug** — 3 × 8/side (low back glued to floor throughout; skip if uncomfortable)
5. **Child's pose with lateral arm reach** — 60 s each side
6. **Thoracic extension over foam roller** — 90 s (support upper-mid back, not lumbar)
7. **Standing leg swings (front/back)** — 15/side × 2 (easy momentum, not forced)
8. **Band pull-aparts** — 3 × 15 (upper back activation; no spinal involvement)
9. **Box breathing** — 4 min: inhale 4 s, hold 2 s, exhale 6 s
