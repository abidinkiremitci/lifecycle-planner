# Nutrition — Week 7 (2026-08-10 to 2026-08-16)

## Weekly Focus

Back injury recovery week. Training volume is significantly reduced — three short upper-body FF sessions, easy row or mobility on Tuesday, bike commute Thursday, easy walk Saturday. Lower output means lower caloric need. Targets drop to **2300 kcal on active/training days** and **2000 kcal on rest/recovery days**. Protein stays fixed at 185 g to fuel tissue repair and prevent lean mass loss during the injury period. Carbs are reduced proportionally; fat is the secondary lever. Do not cut below these targets even if the session felt light — the body is spending energy on back recovery that does not show up on a training log.

## Key Timing Reminders

- **Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri):** 300–400 kcal with 50–60 g carbs + 30–40 g protein, fat <10 g, 60–90 min before session; drink 500 ml water on waking
- **Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri):** 40–50 g protein + 50–60 g carbs within 45 min (smaller post-workout window than previous weeks — session intensity is lower)
- **Pre-row (Tue, Option A):** 300–400 kcal with 40–55 g carbs + 25–35 g protein; 60 min before; skip if doing Option B mobility only
- **Thursday (bike commute):** No structured fuelling — normal meals timed around commute; no need for sports nutrition
- **Hydration:** 3–3.5 L water daily; add ~500 ml per training hour; electrolytes on row and FF days

## Recovery & Injury Notes

- **Protein priority:** 185 g/day is the floor, not the target — hit it every day. Tissue repair (muscle, disc, ligament) is protein-dependent. Distribute evenly across 4–5 meals (35–40 g/meal) for optimal synthesis.
- **Anti-inflammatory note:** Higher omega-3 intake (oily fish, walnuts, flaxseed) and colourful vegetables support back recovery — no macro change needed, just food quality awareness.
- **Do not under-eat on rest days:** 2000 kcal on Thu/Sat/Sun is already a meaningful deficit. Going lower risks slowing recovery and spiking cortisol — the back needs a calm hormonal environment to heal.
- **Scale tracking:** Weight loss may still occur at these targets (~0.5–0.75 kg/week). If weight drops >1 kg this week, add +150 kcal via carbs on training days immediately.
- **Energy signal:** If Monday's session felt unusually flat at 2300 kcal, raise training days to 2400 kcal (add ~25 g carbs pre-workout) for the remainder of the week.
