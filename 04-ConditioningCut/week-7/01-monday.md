## Warm-up (10 min)

- 2 min easy walk in place
- 2 rounds:
  - 10 cat-cow (pain-free range only — stop at the first sign of sharp pain)
  - 10 glute bridges (feet flat, slow squeeze at top — glute activation without back strain)
  - 10 band pull-aparts
  - 8 scapula pull-ups (dead hang on bar, protract/retract only — no full pull yet)
  - 10 shoulder circles each direction
- Hip flexor: standing hip flexor march 10/side
- 10 seated DB overhead press @ 5 kg/hand (prime the shoulder pattern for strength block)

---

## Strength (15 min)

- **Strict pull-ups 4 × 5 @ bodyweight**, rest 90 s
  - Cues: dead hang start, full scapular depression before pulling, chin clearly over bar, 2 s controlled lower — no kipping, no swinging; hanging between sets passively decompresses the lumbar spine
  - Note: No added weight this week — goal is volume, tendon health, and spinal decompression benefit; if all 4 sets feel easy, add a 3 s pause at the top of each rep rather than adding load
- **Seated DB overhead press 3 × 10 @ 15 kg/hand**, rest 90 s
  - Cues: sit tall on bench, core lightly braced without posterior pelvic tuck, press directly overhead, slow 2 s lower without letting elbows flare past 45°
  - Note: Seated position eliminates lumbar compression entirely; if 15 kg/hand is too heavy drop to 12.5 kg and focus on full range — do not sacrifice shoulder ROM for load

---

## Metcon (AMRAP 18 — target 5–7 rounds)

- **16 box step-ups** (8/side, alternating, box ~45 cm) — sole cardio modality; step controlled with full foot contact, no bounce or jump
- **6 strict pull-ups**
- **12 push-ups** (chest to floor, full lockout; keep hips in line — no sagging low back)

**Strategy:** Step-ups at a brisk walking tempo — this replaces running as cardio without spinal impact. Pull-ups and push-ups unbroken as long as possible; break to sets of 3 on pull-ups before form breaks. RPE 7 — maintenance work, not a test. Score every round to track consistency.

## Skill / Core (12 min)

**Pressing sub — no wall HSPU (8 min):**
- Seated DB overhead press 2 × 8 @ 12.5 kg/hand (drop from strength block — cumulative fatigue)
- Floor pike push-ups 3 × 10 (hands on floor, hips high, forehead travels toward floor — shoulder loading without spinal compression; keep core tight throughout)

**Back-safe core (4 min):**
- Bird dog 3 × 8/side (extend opposite arm + leg, hold 2 s, return without rotating hips — abort if low back aches)
- Side plank 3 × 30 s/side (stacked feet or staggered; no hip sag)

## Cool-down (10 min)

- Cat-cow 10 reps slow (pain-free range only)
- Child's pose with arm reach 60 s (gentle lumbar decompression)
- Couch stretch 2 × 45 s/side (hip flexors — shortened from sitting)
- Lat band stretch palm-up 45 s/side (pull-up recovery)
- Thoracic extension over foam roller 60 s (T-spine only — do not extend into lumbar)
- Box breathing 3 min: inhale 4 s, hold 2 s, exhale 6 s
