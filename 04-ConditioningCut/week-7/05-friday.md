## Strength (15 min)

- **Strict chin-ups 4 × 5 @ bodyweight**, rest 90 s
  - Cues: supinated grip (palms facing you), dead hang start, chin clearly over bar, 2 s controlled lower — no kipping; supinated grip increases bicep contribution vs Monday's overhand pull-ups, providing a complementary stimulus across the week
  - Note: Hanging between sets continues to provide gentle spinal decompression
- **Dips 3 × 10 @ bodyweight** (dip bar), rest 90 s
  - Cues: upright torso, elbows tracking back (not flared wide), lower until elbows hit ~90°, press to full lockout; add a 3 s lower if 10 reps feels easy
  - Note: Body dips with upright torso place minimal lumbar demand — safe for back injury at bodyweight

## Metcon (AMRAP 18 — target 5–7 rounds)

- **16 box step-ups** (8/side, ~45 cm, unloaded) — sole cardio modality; controlled, alternating legs
- **6 chin-ups** (strict — same grip as strength block)
- **10 ring push-ups** (rings ~30–35 cm off floor, feet on floor — instability increases chest and shoulder demand vs standard push-ups)

**Strategy:** Same pace as Monday — RPE 7, consistent rounds. If chin-ups feel heavy after the strength block, scale to 4 reps or swap to ring rows. Ring push-up difficulty adjusts by raising or lowering the rings; raise slightly (40–45 cm) in later rounds if shoulders fatigue. Box step-ups remain the steady-state cardio throughout.
