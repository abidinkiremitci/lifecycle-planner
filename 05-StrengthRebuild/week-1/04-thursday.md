# Day 4 — Thursday (2026-08-27)

## Session Overview
- Cycle: 05-StrengthRebuild — Week 1
- Session: Running
- Target: 4 km easy @ ~6:00/km, RPE 5–6
- Focus: Reactivation run — rebuild aerobic base; zone 2 effort only; no tempo or intervals this week

---

## Warm-up (5 min)

- 3 min easy walk/jog — start as a full walk, transition to a jog when breathing settles
- 10 leg swings forward-back/side (hold wall for balance)
- 10 ankle rolls each foot
- 15 calf raises (slow, full range — heel drops below step level if available)
- 1 min easy jog, arrive at running pace naturally

---

## Main Run

- **4 km easy @ ~6:00/km** (target total: ~24 min)
  - Zone 2: RPE 5–6; breathing should be nasal or conversational throughout — if you can't hold a full sentence, slow down
  - This is a base-building run, not a test; focus on relaxed mechanics: upright posture, foot strike under hips, soft landing
  - **Indoor alternative:** 20 min easy row @ ≤2:40/500 m, SR 18 spm

---

## Cool-down (5 min)

- 3 min easy walk
- Wall calf stretch 2 × 45 s/side (lean into wall, back leg straight, heel grounded)
- Seated single-leg hamstring fold 45 s/side
- Kneeling hip flexor lunge stretch 30 s/side
