## Warm-up (10 min)

- 2 min easy row @ rate 18 spm (or 2 min single unders)
- 2 rounds:
  - 8 cat-cow + 6 thoracic rotations/side
  - 10 band pass-throughs
  - 8 scapula pull-ups on bar
  - 10 wrist circles (front rack prep — flex, extend, rotate)
  - 8 deep bodyweight squats (2 s pause at bottom, elbows high)
- Front-rack prep: 2 min elbows-high wall drill × 12 reps + 10 empty bar push press
- Front squat warm-up: 10 reps @ empty bar → 5 reps @ ~45 kg → 3 reps @ ~60 kg

## Strength (20 min)

- **Front Squat 4 × 4 @ ~70 kg (~65–70%)**
  - Estimated 1RM ~100–105 kg based on Cycle 04 W5–6 data; adjust up or down based on feel in the 60 kg warm-up set
  - Cues: elbows high and forward before descent, brace hard through the core, knees out aggressively, lead with elbows driving up on the ascent
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
- **Accessory — weighted strict pull-ups 4 × 4 @ BW + 5 kg**
  - Cues: dead hang start, full scapular depression before pulling, chin clears bar, 2 s controlled lower — rack bar only, no kip
  - Rest: 2:00 between sets

## Metcon (AMRAP 20 — target 10–12 rounds)

- **20 double unders** — sole cardio modality
  - Alt: 40 single unders
- **8 double KB push press** @ 16 kg each
  - Dip-and-drive with both KBs simultaneously; full lockout overhead; guide back to rack, don't drop elbows early
- **10 weighted sit-ups** @ 10 kg plate (held at chest or overhead)

**Strategy:** DU in sets of 20 unbroken or 2 × 10; push press should be unbroken every round at 16 kg; sit-ups continuous steady pace. RPE 7–8. Round time target ~1:40–1:50 min.

## Skill / Core (13 min)

**HSPU — direct wall work (8 min):**
- **3 × 8 kipping HSPU**, rest 90 s between sets
  - Kick up to wall, active shoulders, heels lightly on wall throughout
  - Each rep requires full lockout; stop the set if lockout fails rather than grind out a partial
  - This is a reactivation set — no max effort; 8 controlled reps per set is the target
  - If ankles feel stressed after set 1, reduce to 6 reps per set and note it

**Core (5 min):**
- 3 rounds:
  - 20 s hollow hold (arms overhead, lower back pressed to floor, controlled breathing)
  - 8 dead bugs/side (slow, lower back glued throughout)
  - Rest 15 s

- Wall lat stretch palm-up 45 s/side (pull-up recovery)
