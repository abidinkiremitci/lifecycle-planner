## Warm-up (10 min)

- 2 rounds:
  - 8 cat-cow + 6 hip 90/90 switches/side
  - 10 glute bridges (2 s hold at top, feet flat)
  - 10 band pull-aparts
  - 10 shoulder pass-throughs (stick or empty bar)
- Deadlift warm-up:
  - 10 reps Romanian DL @ empty bar (hip hinge activation — feel the hamstrings)
  - 5 reps conventional DL @ ~60 kg
  - 3 reps @ ~80 kg
- 5 hollow rocks (bracing activation before the pull)

---

## Strength (20 min)

- **Deadlift 4 × 4 @ ~95 kg (~65–70%)**
  - Estimated 1RM ~135–140 kg; adjust based on how 80 kg feels in the warm-up
  - Cues: hips back before initiating pull, neutral spine — no lumbar rounding, big breath brace before each rep, drive the floor away, squeeze glutes and drive hips through at lockout; reset fully between reps
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
- **Accessory — single-arm DB row on bench 3 × 10/side @ 22.5 kg**
  - Set-up: plant supporting knee and ipsilateral hand on bench; spine neutral and parallel to floor
  - Cues: elbow drives back — not out to the side; full shoulder ROM; control the lower on a 2 s count; no rotation through the lower back
  - Rest: 90 s (alternate sides back-to-back within each set)

---

## Metcon (AMRAP 20 — target 9–11 rounds)

- **10 box jump overs** — sole cardio modality (use plyo box; Alt: 10 box step-overs)
  - Land softly with soft knees; step or jump down on the far side; reset foot position before the next jump
- **8 dips** (parallel dip bars; Alt: 8 bench dips if needed)
  - Elbows track back, not flared; lower until upper arms are parallel or slightly below; full lockout at top; upright torso
- **12 hollow rocks**
  - Arms overhead, legs straight, lower back off the floor — maintain the hollow position throughout; if breaking, switch to hollow holds instead

**Strategy:** Box jumps at a controlled rhythm — no rushing between jumps; dips unbroken at W1 loads; hollow rocks in one set. RPE 7–8. Round time target ~1:50–2:00 min.

---

## Skill / Core (12 min)

**DB overhead press, seated on bench (8 min):**
- **3 × 8 @ 15 kg each DB**, rest 90 s between sets
  - Cues: seated upright on bench, core braced, start from shoulder height, press to full lockout directly overhead (no forward lean), 2 s controlled lower back to shoulder height
  - This is the Wednesday pressing modality — no ankle/wall contact; purely overhead volume

**Core (4 min):**
- 3 rounds:
  - 8 banded pallof press/side (band anchored to rack; half-kneeling or standing; resist rotation; hold 2 s at full extension)
  - 10 weighted sit-ups @ 10 kg
  - Rest 15 s
