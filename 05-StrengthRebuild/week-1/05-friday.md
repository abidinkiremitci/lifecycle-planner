## Warm-up (10 min)

- 2 rounds:
  - 8 cat-cow + 6 thoracic rotations/side
  - 10 bodyweight squats (2 s pause at the bottom, knees out)
  - 8 band pull-aparts + 10 scapula pull-ups on bar
  - 10 hip 90/90 switches/side
- Calf activation: 2 × 12 slow calf raises (bodyweight)
- Back squat warm-up: empty bar × 10 → ~50 kg × 5 → ~67.5 kg × 3

---

## Strength (20 min)

- **Back Squat 4 × 4 @ ~82.5 kg (~65–70%)**
  - Estimated 1RM ~120–125 kg; adjust based on how 67.5 kg feels in the warm-up set
  - Cues: big breath brace before descent, elbows up under the bar to keep torso upright, knees track toes aggressively, controlled 2 s descent, drive explosively out of the hole
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
- **Accessory — chin-ups 3 × 5 BW**
  - Supinated grip (palms facing you), dead hang start, chin clears bar, 2 s controlled lower — strict only (rack bar)
  - Rest: 2:00 between sets

---

## Metcon (AMRAP 20 — target 8–10 rounds)

- **12 cal row** — sole cardio modality (Alt: 12 burpees — step-back option available)
  - Drive at a hard-but-sustainable pace; 12 cals should take ~35–45 s; don't sprint — you have 20 min
- **8 hang power cleans** @ 50 kg
  - Start from mid-shin hang (hips back, lats engaged), explosive hip extension, high elbows pulling under, receive in a quarter front rack; reset each rep from the hang
  - If any reps feel technically inconsistent, break into 5 + 3 rather than grinding through
- **12 V-ups**
  - Arms overhead at the start; simultaneously lift legs and torso; touch hands to feet at the top; controlled lower; if breaking, switch to tuck-ups

**Strategy:** Row at a consistent driving pace; hang power cleans at 50 kg should be manageable for unbroken sets in W1 — use the hang as a brief reset between reps; V-ups continuous. RPE 7–8.

## Skill / Core (12 min)

**Ring push-ups (8 min):**
- **3 × 10**, rest 90 s between sets
  - Set rings at approximately chest height when kneeling; adjust height so the push-up is challenging but full reps are achievable
  - Cues: straight body line throughout, elbows track back (not flared), ring faces turn from neutral to slightly pronated at lockout, full lockout at the top
  - W1: rings not turned out aggressively — build comfort with ring stability first

**Core (4 min):**
- L-sit hold on dip bars: 3 × 15 s, rest 30 s between efforts
  - Arms straight, legs parallel to floor (or tucked if parallel is not achievable); squeeze quads and point toes
  - If 15 s is easy, add 5 s per set
