# Nutrition — Week 1 (2026-08-24 to 2026-08-30)

## Weekly Focus

Week 1 is a reactivation week transitioning out of Cycle 04's caloric deficit (~2300 kcal/day) into performance-first fuelling. The significant calorie increase — particularly the carbohydrate jump — will replenish depleted glycogen stores and likely cause a temporary bodyweight increase of 1–2 kg in the first two weeks. This is expected and desirable: muscles refuelling, not fat gain. Prioritise protein consistency at 185 g/day and hit carb targets on training days to support the return to full barbell loading.

## Key Timing Reminders

**Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — 60–90 min before session:**
- ~500 kcal | ~70 g carbs | ~35 g protein | fat <15 g
- 500 ml water on waking

**Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — within 45 min:**
- ~45 g protein + ~70 g carbs | fat minimal (<10 g)

**Pre-row (Tuesday) — 60 min before:**
- ~450 kcal | ~65 g carbs | ~30 g protein | fat <15 g

**Pre-run (Thursday) — 60–90 min before:**
- ~350 kcal | ~55 g carbs | ~25 g protein | fat <10 g (keep fat low to avoid GI issues on the run)

**Hydration:**
- 3.5–4 L water daily baseline
- Add ~500 ml per training hour
- Electrolytes (500–700 mg/L sodium + potassium) on row (Tuesday) and run (Thursday) days

## Tracking Notes

- **Bodyweight:** expect +1–2 kg in W1–2 from glycogen rehydration — track trend over 7–10 days, not daily
- **Energy levels:** if sessions feel flat despite hitting carb targets, add +30 g carbs on training days for one week and reassess
- **Pull-up tracking:** record load and reps for weighted sets on Monday (target: BW+5 kg × 4 × 4 clean)
- **HSPU tracking:** record reps per set and ankle soreness post-wall on Monday
- **Running pace:** log Thursday pace and RPE (W1 target: 4 km @ ~6:00/km, RPE 5–6)
