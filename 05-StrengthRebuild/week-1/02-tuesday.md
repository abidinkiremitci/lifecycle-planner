# Day 2 — Tuesday (2026-08-25)

## Session Overview
- Cycle: 05-StrengthRebuild — Week 1
- Session: Indoor Rowing
- Target: 5 km steady @ ≤2:35/500 m, SR 20 spm
- Focus: Re-establish base aerobic capacity; clean technique throughout; no sprint effort

---

## Warm-up (5 min)

- 3 min easy row @ rate 18 spm — focus on breathing and posture, not pace
- 8 shoulder circles each direction
- 10 leg swings forward-back/side, 8 hip 90/90 switches
- 1 min easy row build: start at 18 spm, step up to 20 spm by the final 30 s

---

## Main Row (5 km)

- **5 km steady state @ target ≤2:35/500 m**
  - Stroke rate: 20 spm throughout
  - Drive sequence: legs fully extend → hip rock → arms pull to lower ribs; catch with straight arms and shins vertical
  - RPE 6–7: comfortably hard; short sentences possible; nasal breathing for the first 2 km
  - Split pacing: hold ≤2:35/500 m from the 500 m mark; don't go out faster than 2:30 in the first 1 km
  - W1 intent is base building — prioritise clean form over split; if split drifts to 2:40, that's fine for today

---

## Cool-down (5 min)

- Kneeling hip flexor stretch 45 s/side
