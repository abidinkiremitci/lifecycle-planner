# Nutrition — Week 4 (2026-09-14 to 2026-09-20)

## Weekly Focus
Week 4 runs a calorie cap of ~2700 kcal across all days for a couple of weeks (athlete-requested). This is a deliberate short-term reduction from the cycle's normal performance-first targets. Protein stays fixed at ~185 g/day to protect muscle mass and pull-up/HSPU strength gains. Fat holds at ~90 g/day. Carbs drop uniformly to ~285–290 g/day — significantly below normal training-day targets — so training output may feel slightly reduced, particularly in longer metcons and tempo running. This is expected and acceptable. If energy drops sharply (RPE feels 1–2 points higher than usual for the same work), add a 20–30 g carb boost in the pre-workout window only and monitor for 2–3 sessions before raising the daily total.

## Key Timing Reminders

**Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — 60–90 min before session:**
- ~450 kcal | ~55–60 g carbs | ~35 g protein | fat <12 g
- 500 ml water on waking
- Keep the pre-workout window tighter than usual given the reduced daily total

**Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — within 45 min:**
- ~40–45 g protein + ~50–60 g carbs | fat minimal (<10 g)
- Prioritise this window — it is the most important use of the available carb budget on training days

**Pre-row (Tuesday) — 60 min before:**
- ~400 kcal | ~55 g carbs | ~28 g protein | fat <12 g

**Pre-run (Thursday) — 60–90 min before:**
- ~350 kcal | ~50 g carbs | ~25 g protein | fat <10 g (keep fat especially low to avoid gut issues on run)

**Hydration:**
- 3.5–4 L water daily baseline
- Add ~500 ml per training hour
- Electrolytes (500–700 mg/L sodium + potassium) on row and run days; do not skip this even under calorie restriction

## Tracking Notes
- **Calorie compliance:** Track all days; the restriction window is ~2 weeks (W4 and W5). If bodyweight is dropping more than ~0.5 kg/week by the end of W4, raise carbs by +30 g/day to slow the deficit rate
- **Energy check:** Flag any session where RPE feels >2 points higher than expected — this signals under-fuelling rather than fitness deficit
- **Pull-up tracking:** Record weighted sets on Monday (4 × 5 @ BW + 7.5 kg) and chin-up reps on Friday (4 × 6 BW); note if fatigue causes early set termination
- **HSPU tracking:** Record reps per set after Monday wall work (target 4 × 10); note ankle soreness rating after each wall session
- **Running pace:** Log Thursday tempo pace and RPE; target 3 × 1 km @ ~5:00/km; flag hip soreness rating — if it exceeds 2/10, scale back tempo volume for W5 running
- **Hip monitoring:** Track hip soreness across Mon / Thu / Fri — if it trends upward through the week, reduce squat loading by 5% in W5 and shift Thursday to the indoor rowing alternative
- **Adjustment rule:** If two consecutive training sessions feel flat despite clean pre-workout nutrition timing, add +30 g carbs to the pre-workout window only (not the full daily target) and monitor before making a permanent change to the daily cap
