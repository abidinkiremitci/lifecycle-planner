## Strength (20 min)
- **Back Squat 4 × 4 @ ~100–102.5 kg (~80–82%)**
  - Cues: big breath brace before the descent, elbows up to keep the torso upright, knees track toes, control the lowering phase, drive powerfully out of the hole
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
- **Accessory — chin-ups 4 × 6 BW**
  - Cues: dead hang start, chin clears the bar, full lockout at the bottom, 2 s controlled lower — strict only, no kip
  - Rest: 2:00 between sets

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 10–12 rounds)
- ⚠️ No squats, lunges, step-ups, or thrusters in this metcon (Back Squat day)

**AMRAP 22:**
- **15 box step-ups** (60 cm box, alternating legs) — sole cardio modality
  - Drive through the heel of the stepping leg; stand fully upright at the top before stepping down
  - Note: box step-ups are a controlled, low-impact movement distinct from box jump overs used on Monday
  - ⚠️ Reduce to step-downs if quad fatigue from Back Squat is significant; focus is cardio output, not loading
- **10 push jerks @ 50 kg**
  - Dip-and-drive, punch under the bar aggressively, fully lock out the arms and legs in the catch; stand to full extension before lowering
- **8 strict pull-ups** (BW — no kip, rack bar)
  - Full dead hang at the bottom; chin over bar; controlled lower; break into 4+4 if needed to maintain quality

**Strategy:** Settle into a 2 min per round rhythm. Step-ups steady and consistent; push jerks smooth and technical; pull-ups quality over speed. RPE 7–8.
