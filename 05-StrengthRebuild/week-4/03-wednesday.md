## Metcon (EMOM 24 — 4 rounds of a 6-min EMOM — target 22–24 min active)
- ⚠️ No KB swings, RDL, or hip-hinge movements in this metcon (Deadlift day)

**EMOM 24 (4 cycles of minutes 1–6):**
- **Min 1:** 50 single unders — sole cardio modality
  - Aim to finish in ≤35 s; if rope is unavailable sub 20 tuck jumps
- **Min 2:** 10 strict push-ups
  - Full lockout at top, chest to floor; break into 2 sets of 5 rather than grind if needed
- **Min 3:** 5 hang power snatches @ 40 kg
  - From mid-thigh hang, explosive hip snap, high pull, lockout overhead; reset between reps
- **Min 4:** 6 DB push press @ 2 × 15 kg
  - Dip-and-drive, press to full lockout overhead simultaneously, controlled lower
- **Min 5:** 8 ring rows
  - Body horizontal; elbows drive back, chest to rings, full lockout at the bottom
- **Min 6:** REST

**Target:** Each working minute finishes with 10–15 s rest. If any minute runs over consistently, reduce reps by 1–2 for that station. RPE 7–8.
