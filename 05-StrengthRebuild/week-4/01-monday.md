## Strength (20 min)
- **Front Squat 4 × 4 @ ~97.5–100 kg (~78–82%)**
  - Cues: elbows high and forward before the descent, brace hard through midline, knees out aggressively, lead with elbows on the way up
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
  - ⚠️ If hip discomfort rises above 2/10 during the warm-up sets, stay at 95 kg and reduce to 3 sets
- **Accessory — weighted strict pull-ups 4 × 5 @ BW + 7.5 kg**
  - Cues: dead hang start, full scapular depression, chin over the bar, 2 s controlled lower — no kip, rack bar only
  - Rest: 2:00 between sets

## Metcon (RFT 5 rounds — target 22–24 min)
- ⚠️ No squats, lunges, step-ups, thrusters, or goblet squats in this metcon (Front Squat day)

**5 Rounds for Time:**
- **10 box jump overs** (60 cm box) — sole cardio modality
  - Two-foot take-off; land on top and step OR jump down the far side; reset and repeat
  - No run alternative needed here; if ankles are uncomfortable, replace with 10 broad jumps (stand, jump forward as far as possible, walk back to start)
- **8 hang power cleans @ 55 kg**
  - From mid-thigh hang, aggressive hip extension, high elbows, receive at parallel; stand to full extension before the next rep
- **10 strict push-ups**
  - Full lockout at the top, chest touches the floor, body stays rigid; break into small sets rather than grinding through poor reps

**Target:** 22–24 min total. Steady 60–90 s rest after each complete round if needed; RPE 8.
