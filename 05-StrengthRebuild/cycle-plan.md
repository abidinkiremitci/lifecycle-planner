# 05-StrengthRebuild – Cycle Plan

## Overview

- **Focus:** Return to full barbell loading after Cycle 04 recovery weeks; build pull-up strength and volume as primary performance goal; develop 10 km running pace through structured Thursday sessions; continue kipping HSPU volume progression after injury gap
- **Duration:** 8 weeks (2026-08-24 to 2026-10-18)
- **Days per week:** 5
  - Mon / Wed / Fri: Functional fitness (strength + metcon + skill/core — 60–70 min)
  - Tue: Indoor rowing (30–60 min, VO2 focus — target 10 km sub-50 min)
  - Thu: Running (30–60 min — target 10 km sub-50 min)
  - Sat / Sun: Rest (one day optional easy active recovery walk)

### Goals

- **Pull-ups (primary):** Progressive weighted strict pull-up loading; max-rep test at BW+10 kg Week 8; accumulate weekly volume across all three rotation modes
- **10 km run pace:** Structured Thursday plan — W1–2 zone-2 base → W5–6 lactate threshold → W7 race-pace intervals → W8 10 km time trial (goal: sub-50 min / ~5:00/km)
- **HSPU:** Exceed 21-rep kipping max-rep set (Cycle 03 baseline); continue ankle-safe wall rotation from Cycle 04
- **Barbell:** Return Front Squat, Deadlift, Back Squat to peak Cycle 04 loads by W4; push beyond by W6
- **Nutrition:** Performance-first — return to full training targets after Cycle 04 deficit

## Programming Rules

These rules apply to every week and must be followed when generating weekly plans.

### 1. Muscle Group Separation

Each day's metcon and accessory must not load the same primary muscle group as the day's strength lift.

| Day | Strength Lift | Primary Muscles Loaded | Metcon Restrictions | Accessory Restrictions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Front Squat | Quads, glutes, anterior chain | No squats, goblet squats, lunges, step-ups, thrusters, wall balls | No squat variations (no goblet, box squat, etc.) |
| Wednesday | Deadlift | Posterior chain, hamstrings, glutes, lower back | No KB swings, RDL, good mornings, or any hip-hinge movement | No hinge variations (no RDL, rack pull, sumo DL, etc.); use single-arm DB row on bench (supported lower back) |
| Friday | Back Squat | Quads, glutes, anterior chain | No squats, lunges, step-ups, thrusters | No squat variations |

### 2. Accessory Must Not Duplicate Strength Exercise

The accessory movement must be a different exercise pattern from the day's main lift. The pull-up/row rotation already satisfies this — do not substitute any squat or hinge variation into Monday/Friday accessories, or any squat into Wednesday accessory.

### 3. Metcon Movement Variety

- Within any given week, no single movement should appear in more than one of the three functional fitness metcons (Mon/Wed/Fri)
- Across consecutive weeks, rotate the cardio modality and primary movers used in metcons — avoid repeating identical structures two weeks in a row
- Only ONE cardio movement per metcon (row, run, burpee, box jump, double unders, devil press, etc.)

### 4. Metcon Hard Restrictions

- No kipping pull-ups, toes-to-bar, or chest-to-bar
- No bench press, strict press, or back squats inside metcons
- No continuous carries > 20 m (space: 5 m × 4 m); use shuttles or in-place movements
- If running is a metcon cardio option → always provide row / jump rope alternative

## Nutrition

Return to performance-first targets (end of Cycle 04 caloric deficit):

| Day Type | Days | Calories | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Functional Fitness | Mon / Wed / Fri | ~3400 kcal | ~185 g | ~460 g | ~90 g |
| Endurance (Row / Run) | Tue / Thu | ~3200 kcal | ~185 g | ~410 g | ~90 g |
| Rest | Sat / Sun | ~2700 kcal | ~185 g | ~290 g | ~90 g |

- Protein fixed at ~185 g/day regardless of day type
- Carbs are the primary lever — increase on high-output days, reduce on rest days
- Fat stays at ~90 g/day; adjust ±10 g only for calorie fine-tuning

Phase adjustments by block:
- **W1–2:** Baseline targets — body readjusting to increased calories after deficit
- **W3–6:** Full targets as above — fuel peak loading and recovery
- **W7–8:** Reduce slightly to ~3200 kcal on FF days (deload / taper week) if body composition feedback warrants
