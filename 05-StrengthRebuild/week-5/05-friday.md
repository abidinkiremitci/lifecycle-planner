## Strength (20 min)
- **Back Squat 4 × 3 @ 105 kg (~84–86%)**
  - Cues: big breath brace before the descent, elbows up under the bar to support the shelf, knees track toes aggressively, control the lowering phase, drive powerfully out of the hole
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
- **Accessory — chin-ups 3 × max-rep @ BW**
  - Cues: dead hang start, chin clears the bar, 2 s controlled lower — strict, no kip
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets (max-rep effort — take full rest)
  - Record each set individually

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 8–10 rounds)
- ⚠️ No squats, lunges, step-ups, thrusters in this metcon (Back Squat day)

**AMRAP 22:**
- **10 box jump overs** (60 cm box) — sole cardio modality
  - Two-foot take-off; land on top and step OR jump down the far side; reset and repeat
  - Keep the rhythm consistent — the rest in this metcon comes from pacing, not stopping
- **8 push jerks @ 47.5–50 kg**
  - Dip-and-drive, punch under aggressively, lock out arms and legs in the catch, stand to full extension before lowering; use 50 kg if W4 push jerks (50 kg) felt manageable
- **6 ring dips**
  - Rings at hip height, full lockout at the top, elbows track back in the dip — stop the set if shoulders flare forward; break into 3+3 if needed

**Strategy:** Aim for a consistent ~2:00–2:30 per round. Box jump overs set the rhythm; press stays technical; ring dips are quality over speed. RPE 7–8.

## Skill / Core (12 min)
**Ring push-ups — max-rep test (8 min):**
- **4 × max-rep**, rest 90 s between sets
  - Rings at chest height; keep the body straight, elbows track back, full lockout at the top — stop the set the moment form breaks
  - Record each set individually; total volume across sets is the metric; aim to beat W4's 3×20 total
  - Note: 4 sets vs W4's 3 sets gives you one extra max-rep opportunity

**Core (4 min):**
- 3 rounds:
  - 8 V-ups (lower back flat, full hip extension at the bottom)
  - :20 s L-sit on dip bars
  - Rest 20 s
