- Stroke rate: 24–26 spm

- Drive: legs fully extend → hips open → arms finish to lower ribs; stay tall, avoid lunging at the catch

**Pacing strategy:**
- 500 m: ease into 2:30–2:32/500 m — do not go out faster; the first 500 m must feel controlled

- 3500–4500 m: hold pace; if breathing is under control, begin a slight negative split
- 4500–5000 m: empty the tank — take the split to 2:22–2:25 if you have anything left
