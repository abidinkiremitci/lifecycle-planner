- 2 rounds:
  - 8 cat-cow + 6 hip 90/90 switches/side
  - 10 glute bridges (bodyweight, 2 s squeeze at top)
  - 10 band pull-aparts
  - 8 shoulder pass-throughs (stick or empty bar)
- Deadlift warm-up:
  - 10 Romanian deadlifts @ empty bar (neutral spine, controlled hinge — warm up the pattern only)
  - 5 conventional deadlifts @ ~80 kg
  - 3 reps @ ~100 kg
  - 2 reps @ ~112.5 kg
- 5 hollow rocks for brace activation

## Strength (20 min)
- **Deadlift 4 × 3 @ 125 kg (~83–85%)**
  - Cues: hips back before initiating the pull, neutral spine throughout, big breath brace before each rep, drive the floor away, squeeze glutes hard at lockout — do not chase reps over position
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
  - If the pull feels heavy in the warm-up at 112.5 kg, do a fourth warm-up single at 115 kg before the working sets
- **Accessory — BB bent-over row 4 × 10 @ 72.5–75 kg**
  - Cues: neutral spine, row through the elbows not the hands, bar traces close to the body, 2 s controlled lower — protect the lower back by keeping the hinge angle consistent and not rocking
  - Rest: 90 s between sets

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 6–8 rounds)
- ⚠️ No KB swings, RDL, hip-hinge movements in this metcon (Deadlift day)

**AMRAP 22:**
- **6 burpees to pull-up bar** — sole cardio modality
  - Perform a standard burpee, then jump to the pull-up bar and touch it with both hands at the top — no pull required; this is a controlled jump
  - Keep burpees rhythmic and consistent; rest comes from slowing the pace, not stopping
- **8 BB push press @ 45 kg**
  - Dip-and-drive, press to full lockout overhead simultaneously, 2 s controlled lower back to front rack
  - Break into 4+4 from round 4 if needed to maintain shoulder position
- **10 ring rows**
  - Body horizontal, elbows drive back, chest touches the rings, full lockout at the bottom; scale angle to make this moderate — feet forward = harder

**Target:** 6–8 rounds at a controlled pace. Each round should take ~2:30–3:00. RPE 7–8.

## Skill / Core (12 min)
**DB overhead press — seated on bench (8 min):**
- **4 × 10 @ 22.5 kg each DB**, rest 90 s between sets
  - Seated upright on bench, core braced, press to full lockout directly overhead, 2 s controlled lower — this is the first week at 22.5 kg; treat reps 8–10 as genuinely challenging
  - Stop the set at 8 reps if lockout degrades — record what you get

**Core (4 min):**

  - 10 weighted sit-ups @ 10 kg plate
  - Rest 20 s
