# Nutrition — Week 5 (2026-09-21 to 2026-09-27)

## Weekly Focus
Week 5 tightens the calorie cap from 2700 kcal (W4) to **2600 kcal flat across all days** at athlete request. This deepens the deficit and will further reduce training-day carb availability. Protein (185 g) and fat (~90 g) remain fixed — carbs drop to approximately 262 g/day. At this intake level on FF training days the shortfall versus normal performance targets (~460 g carbs) is significant, so managing the pre-workout window becomes the highest-priority lever: front-loading as many of the daily carbs as possible in the 90 min before each session will protect HSPU test quality, weighted pull-up performance, and peak barbell loading. Monday's HSPU max-rep test and Tuesday's 5 km row time trial are the week's key performance moments — do not sacrifice carbs from those pre-workout windows.

## Key Timing Reminders

**Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — 60–90 min before session:**
- ~400–450 kcal | ~50–60 g carbs | ~30–35 g protein | fat <12 g
- 500 ml water on waking
- This is the most important use of the daily carb budget — do not skip or undercut this window

**Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — within 45 min:**
- ~35–40 g protein + ~45–55 g carbs | fat minimal (<10 g)
- Keep this window tight and clean; it is the second-highest carb priority of the day

**Pre-row (Tuesday) — 60 min before time trial:**
- ~400 kcal | ~55–60 g carbs | ~28–32 g protein | fat <12 g
- The row TT is the key performance test this week — do not go into it fasted or with low carbs

**Pre-run (Thursday) — 60–90 min before:**
- ~300–350 kcal | ~40–50 g carbs | ~25 g protein | fat <10 g
- Thursday is easy zone 2 — the pre-run window can be lighter than FF days; keep fat very low to avoid gut issues

**Hydration:**
- 3.5–4 L water daily baseline
- Add ~500 ml per training hour
- Electrolytes (500–700 mg/L sodium + potassium) on Tuesday (row TT) and Thursday (run); do not skip even under calorie restriction

## Tracking Notes
- **Calorie compliance:** Track all days. If bodyweight drops more than ~0.5 kg/week across W4+W5, raise carbs by +30–40 g/day for one week before continuing the restriction
- **Energy check:** If Monday's HSPU test or weighted pull-ups feel significantly weaker than W4 despite clean pre-workout timing, the 2600 kcal cap is hitting performance — flag immediately rather than pushing through
- **Pull-up tracking:** Record all weighted sets Monday (4×4 @ BW+10 kg) and chin-up max-rep sets Friday; note if any set ends earlier than expected due to grip or fatigue
- **HSPU tracking:** Record each max-rep set separately (3 attempts); compare total reps to W4's 40 reps (4×10)
- **Rowing time trial:** Record 5 km time, average split, stroke rate, and RPE — this is the baseline for all future row benchmarks
- **Running / hip tracking:** Log Thursday hip soreness before and after the run (0–10 scale); if it trends above 3/10 during the session, log it as a flag for W6 adjustments
- **Adjustment rule:** If two consecutive sessions feel flat despite hitting the pre-workout carb window, raise daily carbs by +30 g (keeping calories at ~2720) and monitor for one week before deciding whether to reduce the overall cap
- **Deficit reminder:** 2600 kcal at 185 g protein and 90 g fat leaves ~262 g carbs — this is a genuine deficit for a 92 kg athlete training 5×/week; results should include ~0.5 kg/week fat loss if compliance is consistent
