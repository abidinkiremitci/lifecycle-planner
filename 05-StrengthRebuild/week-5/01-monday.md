## Strength (20 min)
- **Front Squat 4 × 4 @ 97 kg (~78–80%)**
  - Cues: elbows high and forward before the descent, big breath brace, knees out aggressively, lead with elbows on the drive — same weight as W4, focus on bar speed and crisp positions
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
- **Accessory — weighted strict pull-ups 4 × 4 @ BW + 10 kg**
  - Cues: dead hang start, full scapular depression, chin over the bar, 2 s controlled lower — no kip, rack bar only
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets (heavier load — take the full rest)
  - Note: first rep at this load may feel stiff; if the third or fourth rep is a grind, reduce to BW+7.5 kg and finish the sets with quality

## Metcon (RFT 4 rounds — target 20–22 min)
- ⚠️ No squats, lunges, step-ups, thrusters in this metcon (Front Squat day)

**4 Rounds for Time:**
- **40 double unders** — sole cardio modality
  - Unbroken target in rounds 1–2; break into 20+20 from round 3 if needed; aim to finish DU in ≤35 s
  - Alt (no rope): 25 box jump overs (60 cm, step down) or 80 single unders
- **8 hang power cleans @ 60 kg**
  - From mid-thigh hang, aggressive hip extension, high elbows; receive just above parallel; stand fully before the next rep
  - Break into 4+4 if needed from round 3
- **8 strict push-ups**
  - Full lockout at top, chest to floor, rigid body; split 4+4 rather than grinding
- **6 strict pull-ups** (BW, rack bar)
  - Dead hang start, chin clears the bar, controlled lower — no kip; reduce to 4 reps if grip is fading

**Target:** 20–22 min total. 60–90 s deliberate rest after each complete round; RPE 8.
