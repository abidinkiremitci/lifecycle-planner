# Day 5 — Friday (2026-09-11)

## Session Overview
- Cycle: 05-StrengthRebuild — Week 3
- Session: Functional Fitness — Back Squat
- Load target: ~75–80%
- Metcon target: 20–22 min
- Focus: Increase squat loading and maintain strength quality while finishing the week with a varied, high-output metcon and ring push-up strength

## Notes
- Back Squat target this week is ~97.5–100 kg, a clean step above W2 at ~95 kg
- Friday chin-up progression is 4 × 5 strict chin-ups; keep tempo crisp and stop if you lose full extension at the bottom
- Metcon must not contain squats, lunges, step-ups, or thrusters (Back Squat day)
- No movement in this metcon repeats Monday's or Wednesday's cardio movement
- Nutrition: ~3400 kcal today (FF day)

## Warm-up (10 min)
- 2 rounds:
  - 8 cat-cow + 6 thoracic rotations/side
  - 10 bodyweight squats (2 s pause at the bottom)
  - 8 band pull-aparts + 10 scapula pull-ups on the bar
  - 10 hip 90/90 switches/side
- Calf activation: 2 × 12 slow calf raises
- Back squat warm-up: empty bar × 10 → ~55 kg × 5 → ~75 kg × 3 → ~85 kg × 2

---

## Strength (20 min)
- **Back Squat 4 × 4 @ ~97.5–100 kg (~75–80%)**
  - Cues: big breath brace before the descent, elbows up to keep the torso upright, knees track toes, control the lowering phase, drive powerfully out of the hole
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
- **Accessory — chin-ups 4 × 5 BW**
  - Cues: dead hang start, chin clears the bar, full lockout at the top, 2 s controlled lower — strict only, rack bar, no kip
  - Rest: 2:00 between sets

---

## Metcon (AMRAP 18–20 — target 9–10 rounds)
- ⚠️ No squats, lunges, step-ups, or thrusters in this metcon (Back Squat day)
- **12 burpees to pull-up bar** — sole cardio modality
  - Step-back option is acceptable if fatigue rises, but keep the movement continuous and repeatable
  - Goal is to finish each set without stopping the rhythm
- **6 power clean + push jerk singles @ 50 kg**
  - Each rep is one power clean followed by one push jerk, resetting fully between reps; stand tall and lock out the jerk before the next rep

**Strategy:** Burpees at a consistent pace; power clean + push jerk singles stay smooth and precise, one rep at a time. RPE 7–8. Round time target ~1:55–2:10 min.

---

## Skill / Core (12 min)

- **4 × 10**, rest 90 s between sets

**Core (4 min):**

  - 8 dead bugs/side (slow, lower back flat)
  - 8 hollow rocks
  - Rest 15 s
