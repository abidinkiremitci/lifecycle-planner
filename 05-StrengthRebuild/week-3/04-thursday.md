# Day 4 — Thursday (2026-09-10)

## Session Overview
- Cycle: 05-StrengthRebuild — Week 3
- Session: Running
- Target: 6 km total with 2 × 1.5 km tempo @ ~5:10/km, plus easy recovery blocks between
- Focus: Increase aerobic density through longer tempo segments while maintaining a controlled effort before Friday squats

## Notes
- W2 was 5 km with 2 × 1 km tempo; W3 extends the tempo portions to 1.5 km and keeps the total volume at 6 km
- The easy blocks remain deliberate so the session feels strong rather than chopped up
- If weather is poor, use the rower as an indoor alternative: 5 × 1 km @ ≤2:25/500 m with 2 min easy row between pieces
- Nutrition: ~3200 kcal today (Endurance day)

## Warm-up (5 min)
- 3 min easy walk/jog — settle breathing before increasing cadence
- 10 leg swings forward-back/side (hold wall for balance)
- 10 ankle rolls each foot
- 15 calf raises (slow, full range)
- 1 min easy jog build

---

## Main Run (6 km, ~32–34 min)
- **Structure:**
  - **1.5 km easy @ ~6:00/km** (RPE 5)
  - **1.5 km tempo @ ~5:10/km** (RPE 7–8)
  - **1.0 km easy @ ~6:00/km** (RPE 5)
  - **1.5 km tempo @ ~5:10/km** (RPE 7–8)
  - **0.5 km easy @ ~6:00/km** (RPE 4–5)
- Tempo target: 5:10/km (range 5:00–5:20 acceptable)
- Easy blocks should stay smooth and relaxed; do not accelerate to chase the pace on the recovery segments
- **Indoor alternative:** 5 × 1 km @ ≤2:25/500 m, 2 min easy row between pieces

---

## Cool-down (5 min)
- 3 min easy walk
- Wall calf stretch 2 × 45 s/side
- Seated single-leg hamstring fold 45 s/side
- Kneeling hip flexor lunge stretch 30 s/side
