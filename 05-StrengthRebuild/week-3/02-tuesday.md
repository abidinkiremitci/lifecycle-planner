# Day 2 — Tuesday (2026-09-08)

## Session Overview
- Cycle: 05-StrengthRebuild — Week 3
- Session: Indoor Rowing
- Target: 5 × 1000 m @ ≤2:27/500 m, rest 90 s easy row, SR 24–26 spm
- Focus: Build pace control and efficiency under slightly higher training load; move from steady-state coaching to interval quality

## Notes
- W2 was a steady-state 6 km or an interval session; this week shifts into row-pace work per the cycle plan
- Goal is to keep each 1000 m at or under 2:27/500 m while holding a strong, repeatable stroke rate
- Use a controlled start in piece 1 — do not sprint the first 500 m and fade on the later lengths
- Nutrition: ~3200 kcal today (Endurance day)

---

## Warm-up (5 min)
- 3 min easy row @ rate 18 spm — settle posture, breathing, and rhythm
- 8 shoulder circles each direction
- 10 leg swings forward-back/side
- 8 hip 90/90 switches/side
- 1 min easy row build to rate 20 spm

---

## Main Row (20–25 min)
- **5 × 1000 m @ ≤2:27/500 m**
- Rest: 90 s easy row @ rate 16–18 spm between pieces
- Stroke rate: 24–26 spm during work
- Drive: legs extend fully → hips open → arms finish to lower ribs; stay tall through the torso and avoid rushing the recovery
- RPE: 8–8.5 during work, easy on the recoveries

**Pacing target:** hold the first piece controlled and consistent. If the first 500 m is too quick, ease back slightly and let the final 1–2 pieces finish stronger. The average split should be at or below 2:27/500 m.

---

## Cool-down (5 min)
- 2 min easy row @ rate 16–18 spm
- Wall lat stretch palm-up 45 s/side
- Kneeling hip flexor stretch 45 s/side
- Seated forward fold 45 s
