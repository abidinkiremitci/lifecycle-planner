# Nutrition — Week 3 (2026-09-07 to 2026-09-13)

## Weekly Focus
Week 3 is the first full load-increase block of the cycle. The athlete should be fully adapted to the re-entry calories after W1–2 and now is able to support the higher barbell and running demands. Fueling needs to stay consistent, especially around Monday/Wednesday/Friday functional fitness sessions and Thursday's tempo run. If bodyweight or energy is trending down despite hitting the full targets, increase carbs by +30–50 g on the next training day and monitor for one week before making a larger change.

## Key Timing Reminders
**Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — 60–90 min before session:**
- ~500 kcal | ~70 g carbs | ~35 g protein | fat <15 g
- 500 ml water on waking

**Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — within 45 min:**
- ~45 g protein + ~70 g carbs | fat minimal (<10 g)

**Pre-row (Tuesday) — 60 min before:**
- ~450 kcal | ~65 g carbs | ~30 g protein | fat <15 g

**Pre-run (Thursday) — 60–90 min before:**
- ~350 kcal | ~55 g carbs | ~25 g protein | fat <10 g

**Hydration:**
- 3.5–4 L water daily baseline
- Add ~500 ml per training hour
- Electrolytes (500–700 mg/L sodium + potassium) on row and run days

---

## Tracking Notes
- **Bodyweight:** track the 7-day rolling average; if it begins to drift lower than expected, confirm that training-day carbs are not under-target
- **Energy:** if any FF day feels flat, add +30 g carbs to the pre-workout window and monitor the next two training days before changing the full weekly targets
- **Pull-up tracking:** record weighted sets on Monday and chin-up reps on Friday; aim for quality over additional load when fatigue is elevated
- **HSPU tracking:** record reps and ankle soreness after Monday wall work; if the ankle discomfort exceeds 2/10, reduce set size and reassess recovery
- **Running pace:** log Thursday tempo splits separately; target 1.5 km tempo efforts at ~5:10/km with strong rhythm and minimal fade
