## Warm-up (10 min)
- 2 min easy row @ rate 18 spm or 2 min double unders at easy pace
- 2 rounds:
  - 8 cat-cow + 6 thoracic rotations/side
  - 10 band pull-aparts
  - 8 scapular pull-ups on the bar
  - 10 wrist circles (flex/extend/rotate)
  - 8 deep bodyweight squats (2 s pause at bottom)
- Front rack prep: 10 empty bar front squats + 8 barbell overhead holds with 2 sec pause at lockout
- Front squat warm-up: 5 reps @ ~55 kg → 3 reps @ ~75 kg → 2 reps @ ~85 kg

---

## Strength (20 min)
- **Front Squat 4 × 4 @ ~92.5–95 kg (~75–80%)**
  - Cues: elbows high and forward before the descent, brace hard through midline, knees out aggressively, lead with elbows on the way up
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets
- **Accessory — weighted strict pull-ups 4 × 4 @ BW + 7.5 kg**
  - Cues: dead hang start, full scapular depression, chin over the bar, 2 s controlled lower — no kip, rack bar only
  - Rest: 2:00 between sets

---

## Metcon (AMRAP 20 — target 9–11 rounds)
- ⚠️ No squats, lunges, step-ups, thrusters, or goblet squats in this metcon (Front Squat day)
- **10 devil presses** — sole cardio modality
  - 2 × 15 kg DBs or 2 × 12.5 kg DBs if shoulder fatigue is higher than expected
  - Alternate arms smoothly, keep the bell close to the body; finish each rep with a full lockout overhead
- **8 ring dips**
  - Elbows track back, lower until upper arms are just below parallel, full lockout at the top
- **6 push press @ 40 kg**
  - Use leg drive to accelerate the bar overhead; lower under control to the shoulders before the next rep

**Strategy:** 2–3 solid rounds at a sustainable pace, then settle into a steady rhythm. Devil press sits at the front of each round; ring dips and push press should stay unbroken early. RPE 7–8. Round time target ~1:45–2:00 min.

---

## Skill / Core (13 min)
**HSPU — direct wall work (8 min):**
- **4 × 8 kipping HSPU**, rest 90 s between sets
  - Kick up to wall, active shoulders, heels lightly touching the wall, full lockout each rep
  - Stop the set if lockout fails or ankles protest

**Core (5 min):**
- 3 rounds:
  - :20 s L-sit hold on dip bars or bench-supported L-sit
  - 10 weighted sit-ups @ 10 kg plate held at chest
  - Rest 15 s
