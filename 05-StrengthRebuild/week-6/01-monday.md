## Strength (20 min)
- **Front Squat 4 × 3 @ 100 kg (~80–83%)**
  - Cues: elbows high before the descent, brace hard through the trunk, knees out, drive the floor away and lead up with the elbows.
  - Rest: 2:30–3:00 between sets
  - If rep 2 of set 1 is a grind, drop to 97.5 kg and keep all 4 sets crisp.
- **Accessory — weighted strict pull-ups 4 × 5 @ BW + 10 kg**
  - Cues: dead hang start, full scapular depression first, chin clearly over the bar, 2 s controlled lower.
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 6–8 rounds)
- ⚠️ No squats, lunges, step-ups, thrusters, or wall balls in this metcon (Front Squat day)

**AMRAP 22:**
- **8 burpees** — sole cardio modality
  - Chest and thighs to floor, jump to full extension, settle immediately into the barbell.
- **10 hang power snatches @ 40 kg**
  - From mid-thigh, explosive extension, punch to lockout, reset on the hang each rep; break 5+5 early if grip starts to slide.
- **12 abmat sit-ups**
  - Touch behind the head each rep, sit tall at the top, steady breathing.

**Target:** Smooth 2:45–3:15 rounds for 6–8 total rounds. RPE 7.5–8. This should feel longer and more honest than W5 Monday.
