## Strength (20 min)
- **Back Squat 4 × 3 @ 107.5 kg (~85–87%)**
  - Cues: big breath brace, create the upper-back shelf before unracking, knees track toes, stay patient in the bottom, drive hard through mid-foot.
  - Rest: 2:30–3:00 between sets
  - If set 1 loses depth or speed, stay at 105 kg and own the volume.
- **Accessory — chin-ups 3 × max-rep @ BW**
  - Cues: dead hang each rep, chin clearly over the bar, 2 s controlled lower, stop 1 rep before ugly grinding.
  - Rest: 2:30 between sets

## Metcon (AMRAP 22 — target 7–9 rounds)
- ⚠️ No squats, lunges, step-ups, or thrusters in this metcon (Back Squat day)

**AMRAP 22:**
- **45 double unders** — sole cardio modality
  - Aim to finish in ≤35 s. If misses stack up, switch to 70 single unders for the remainder.
- **8 push jerks @ 47.5 kg**
  - Dip-and-drive, punch under fast, stand to full extension before lowering back to the rack.
- **12 V-ups**
  - Lower back stays controlled to the floor each rep; exhale hard at the top.

**Target:** 2:20–2:50 per round for 7–9 rounds. RPE 7.5–8. Rope should raise the heart rate without trashing the legs after squats.
