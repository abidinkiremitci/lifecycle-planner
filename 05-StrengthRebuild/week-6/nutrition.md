# Nutrition — Week 6 (2026-09-28 to 2026-10-04)

## Weekly Focus
Week 6 is still a peak-loading week, but the athlete is staying on the **2600 kcal flat cap** introduced in Week 5. That keeps performance fuel below the cycle's ideal full-training targets, so the job this week is to protect the highest-value sessions rather than pretend recovery resources are unlimited. Priority order: Monday HSPU + weighted pull-ups, Tuesday rowing intervals, Wednesday deadlift, then Friday squat/metcon. Thursday's run is controlled and continuous, so it does not need the same carb aggression as Monday or Tuesday. Protein stays fixed, fats stay stable, and the limited carb budget should be pushed toward the pre- and post-workout windows.

## Key Timing Reminders

**Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — 60–90 min before session:**
- ~400–500 kcal | ~55–65 g carbs | ~30–35 g protein | fat <12 g
- 500 ml water on waking
- Monday and Wednesday are the highest-priority fueling windows this week

**Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — within 45 min:**
- ~35–45 g protein + ~45–60 g carbs | fat minimal (<10 g)
- Get this in even if appetite is low

**Pre-row (Tuesday) — 60 min before intervals:**
- ~400–450 kcal | ~55–65 g carbs | ~28–32 g protein | fat <10–12 g
- Add electrolytes before starting; this is the week's hardest endurance session

**Pre-run (Thursday) — 60–90 min before:**
- ~300–350 kcal | ~40–50 g carbs | ~20–25 g protein | fat <10 g
- Keep the meal light and low-fiber if you plan to run continuously

**Hydration:**
- 3.5–4 L water daily baseline
- Add ~500 ml per training hour
- Electrolytes (500–700 mg/L sodium) on Tuesday and Thursday; use them Monday too if the garage is hot

## Tracking Notes
- **Pull-up tracking:** Record every Monday weighted set at BW+10 kg. Week 6 target is 20 total reps.
- **HSPU tracking:** Record all 4 Monday max-rep sets. Compare total to W5's 37 reps and note the best single set.
- **Deadlift recovery:** If Wednesday deadlift grip or lockout feels notably worse than W5, the calorie cap is likely biting into recovery.
- **Rowing pace:** Tuesday should be smoother than a time trial — track whether all 4 reps stay inside the 2:20–2:25 band without rate creep.
- **Running / hip tracking:** Log hip soreness before, during, and after Thursday. If it rises above 3/10 again, Week 7 should stay conservative.
- **Adjustment rule:** If two peak sessions in the same week feel flat despite hitting the pre-workout carb window, raise training-day carbs by +30–50 g next week before cutting volume.
