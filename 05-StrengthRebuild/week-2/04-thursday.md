- 10 ankle rolls each foot
- 15 calf raises (slow, full range — heel drops below step level if available)

## Main Run (5 km, ~30 min)

- **Structure:**
  - **1 km easy @ ~6:00/km** (RPE 5 — warm into the run, settle breathing)
  - **1 km tempo @ ~5:20/km** (RPE 7–8 — controlled effort; upright posture; foot strike under hips)
  - **1 km easy @ ~6:00/km** (RPE 5 — active recovery between tempo km; resume nasal breathing)
  - **1 km tempo @ ~5:20/km** (RPE 7–8 — aim to match or beat the first tempo km)
  - **1 km easy @ ~6:00/km** (RPE 4–5 — cool-down jog; let effort come down naturally)
- Tempo km target: 5:20/km (5:10–5:30 acceptable range)
- Zone-2 km target: ~6:00/km — do not rush these; recovery quality between tempo efforts matters
- **Indoor alternative:** 5 × (2 min @ ≤2:20/500 m / 2 min easy @ ≤2:45/500 m) on the rower
