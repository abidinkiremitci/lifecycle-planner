## Metcon (AMRAP 20 — target 9–11 rounds)

- ⚠️ No squats, lunges, step-ups, thrusters, or goblet squats in this metcon (Front Squat day)
- **200m run** — sole cardio modality
  - Alt: 500m row if outdoor space is unavailable (or 5 × 40 m out-and-back shuttle run)
- **8 ring dips**
  - Elbows track back (not flared), lower until upper arms are at or slightly below parallel, full lockout at top; upright torso
  - Alt: 8 dip-bar dips if rings are unavailable
- **10 KB swings @ 20 kg** (full overhead — hip hinge is permissible on Front Squat day)
  - Snap hips — not lower back; neutral spine throughout; guide the bell to the midline on the descent; finish directly overhead with arms by ears

**Strategy:** 200m at a controlled pace (~55–65 s); ring dips unbroken in rounds 1–6, break to 5+3 as needed; KB swings continuous. RPE 7–8. Round time target ~1:50–2:00 min.

## Skill / Core (13 min)

**HSPU — direct wall work (8 min):**
- **3 × 10 kipping HSPU**, rest 90 s between sets
  - W2 target per cycle plan (up from 3×8 in W1)
  - Kick up to wall, active shoulders, heels lightly on wall throughout
  - Each rep requires full lockout; stop the set if lockout fails rather than grind out partials
  - If ankles feel stressed after set 1, reduce to 8 reps per set and note ankle soreness out of 10

**Core (5 min):**

  - :20 s L-sit hold on dip bars (legs parallel to floor; tuck-sit if parallel is not yet achievable; press shoulders down and forward)
  - 10 weighted sit-ups @ 10 kg plate (held at chest)
  - Rest 15 s
