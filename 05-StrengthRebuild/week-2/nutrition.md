# Nutrition — Week 2 (2026-08-31 to 2026-09-06)

## Weekly Focus

Week 2 continues the W1–2 baseline reactivation phase after Cycle 04's caloric deficit. The body should be better adjusted to the increased calorie intake now — glycogen stores are more fully replenished than W1 and training output should feel more supported. Any initial bodyweight increase from W1 (1–2 kg from glycogen rehydration) should begin stabilising by mid-week; this is expected and not fat gain. Maintain full carb targets on all training days without reduction. This is the last week at the W1–2 baseline before transitioning to the full performance-first peak targets for W3–6. Metcons return to full completion this week — ensure pre- and post-workout nutrition windows are hit on all three FF days.

## Key Timing Reminders

**Pre-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — 60–90 min before session:**
- ~500 kcal | ~70 g carbs | ~35 g protein | fat <15 g
- 500 ml water on waking

**Post-workout (Mon / Wed / Fri) — within 45 min:**
- ~45 g protein + ~70 g carbs | fat minimal (<10 g)

**Pre-row (Tuesday) — 60 min before:**
- ~450 kcal | ~65 g carbs | ~30 g protein | fat <15 g

**Pre-run (Thursday) — 60–90 min before:**
- ~350 kcal | ~55 g carbs | ~25 g protein | fat <10 g (keep fat low — tempo km demand is higher than W1 easy run; fat slows digestion and may cause GI discomfort)

**Hydration:**
- 3.5–4 L water daily baseline
- Add ~500 ml per training hour
- Electrolytes (500–700 mg/L sodium + potassium) on row (Tuesday) and run (Thursday) days

## Tracking Notes

- **Bodyweight:** track 7-day rolling average, not daily; W1 increase should be stabilising — if still rising in W2, recheck carb sources (favour starchy whole foods over liquid/refined carbs)
- **Energy:** if any metcon (all three must be completed this week) feels flat despite hitting carb targets, add +30 g carbs to the pre-workout window for that day and reassess across the week
- **Pull-up tracking:** record load and reps for weighted sets on Monday (target: BW+5 kg × 4 × 5)
- **HSPU tracking:** record reps per set and ankle soreness out of 10 after Monday wall work (target: 3 × 10)
- **Running tempo:** log Thursday tempo km 1 and km 2 splits separately; target ~5:20/km for both
