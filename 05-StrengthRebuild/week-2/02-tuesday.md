## Warm-up (5 min)

- 3 min easy row @ rate 18 spm — focus on posture and breathing, not pace
- 8 shoulder circles each direction
- 10 leg swings forward-back/side, 8 hip 90/90 switches
- 1 min easy row build: step up from 18 → 20 spm by the final 30 s

---

## Main Row — choose one option

### Option A — Steady State (6 km)
- **6 km continuous @ target ≤2:25/500 m**
- Stroke rate: 20–22 spm throughout
- Drive sequence: legs extend fully → hip rock → arms pull to lower ribs; catch with straight arms and shins vertical
- RPE 6–7: comfortably hard; short sentences possible; aim for nasal breathing through the first 2 km
- Pacing: don't open below 2:10/500 m in km 1; build progressively; target a negative split across the 6 km

### Option B — Intervals (4 × 1000 m)
- **4 × 1000 m @ ≤2:20/500 m**, rest 90 s easy row (rate 16–18 spm) between pieces
- Stroke rate during work: 22–24 spm
- RPE during work: 8–8.5
- Pacing: hold consistent splits across all 4 pieces — do not sprint piece 1 and fade on piece 4
